# Conflict of Interest Policy

**Effective date:** 2025-07-01
**Owning department:** Company-wide
**Classification:** INTERNAL

## 1. Purpose

Acme Corp expects every purchasing decision to be made solely in the company's interest. This policy explains what a conflict of interest is, how it must be declared and how it is managed, so that vendor selection, approvals and contract management remain fair and can be defended to customers, auditors and regulators.

## 2. Scope

The policy applies to all employees, contractors and temporary staff in every department. It is especially relevant to anyone who specifies requirements, evaluates quotes or tenders, approves purchase requests, manages contracts or confirms receipt of goods and services.

## 3. Rules

### 3.1 What counts as a conflict

A conflict of interest exists when an employee's personal interests, or those of a close relative or household member, could influence, or appear to influence, a business decision. Examples include:

- owning shares, other than through a broadly diversified fund, in a vendor or bidder;
- being employed by, advising or receiving payments from a vendor;
- a close relative working for a vendor in a sales or management role;
- receiving gifts, hospitality or other benefits from a vendor.

### 3.2 Declaration of financial interests

Employees must declare any financial interest in a vendor before participating in its selection. Declarations are made through the conflict of interest form in the HR system and must be updated within 10 business days of any change. In addition, every employee who approves purchase requests confirms an annual declaration each January, even if they have nothing to declare.

### 3.3 Managing a declared conflict

Once a conflict is declared, the employee's department manager, with advice from Legal where needed, decides on the measures required. Typical measures are:

- removing the employee from the evaluation panel or approval chain for that vendor;
- assigning an alternative approver for the affected purchase requests, consistent with the Purchase Approval Matrix;
- recording the conflict and the measures taken on the sourcing file.

An employee with a declared conflict must not approve a purchase request involving the affected vendor at any level.

### 3.4 Gifts and hospitality

Gifts from vendors above USD 100 in value must be declared and may not be accepted during an active tender. Further rules apply:

- Cash, gift cards and cash equivalents may not be accepted in any amount.
- Gifts up to USD 100 may be accepted if they are occasional and not linked to a decision; employees should still mention them to their manager.
- Business meals and hospitality must be modest and infrequent; hospitality with a value above USD 100 per person must be declared.
- During an active tender, employees involved in the tender may not accept any gift or hospitality from a participating bidder, whatever its value.

Declared gifts that are not accepted must be politely returned, with a note referring to this policy.

### 3.5 Personal purchases from vendors

Employees may not use their position to obtain personal discounts or benefits from Acme vendors that are not available to all employees under a company-wide arrangement.

## 4. Responsibilities

- **Employees** identify and declare conflicts promptly and step back from affected decisions.
- **Department managers** review declarations, decide on measures and ensure affected employees are removed from relevant decisions.
- **Finance** checks that approvers on high-value requests have no recorded conflict with the selected vendor.
- **Legal** advises on complex conflicts and maintains the gift and conflict register.
- **HR** maintains the declaration form and handles disciplinary matters arising from breaches.

## 5. Exceptions

Exceptions to the gift rules, for example a ceremonial gift that would cause offence to refuse, require the written approval of Legal and must be recorded in the gift register; such gifts become Acme property. There are no exceptions to the requirement to declare financial interests. Failure to declare a conflict may result in disciplinary action and, where a purchase was affected, cancellation of the order.

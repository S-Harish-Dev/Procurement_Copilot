# Contract Management Procedure

**Effective date:** 2026-01-15
**Owning department:** Company-wide
**Classification:** INTERNAL

## 1. Purpose

This procedure sets out how Acme Corp prepares, reviews, signs, stores and manages contracts with vendors. It ensures that contracts protect Acme's interests, that obligations and renewal dates are tracked, and that Acme does not become bound by unfavourable or unplanned renewals.

## 2. Scope

The procedure applies to all agreements under which Acme buys goods or services, including master services agreements, statements of work, software and SaaS agreements, framework agreements, amendments, renewals and online terms accepted on Acme's behalf. It applies to all departments.

## 3. Procedure

### 3.1 Contract owners

Every contract has a named contract owner, normally the employee or department manager responsible for the vendor relationship. The contract owner is accountable for the contract from signature to expiry, including performance, spend, obligations and renewal decisions.

### 3.2 Templates

Contracts should be based on Acme's standard templates wherever possible, as described in the Standard Contract Templates Guide. Vendor paper may be used where the vendor will not accept Acme's template, but it must then be reviewed against Acme's standard positions.

### 3.3 Legal review

Contracts above USD 100,000 require Legal review before signature. Contract value means the total committed value over the full contract term, including renewals that Acme cannot cancel without penalty. Legal review is also required, regardless of value, for contracts that:

- involve the processing of personal data or customer data;
- contain unlimited liability, exclusivity or non-standard indemnities;
- are governed by the law of a country where Acme has no presence;
- include automatic price increases above 5% per year.

Legal aims to complete reviews within 5 business days of receiving a complete request.

### 3.4 Approval and signature

A contract may be signed only after the related purchase request has been approved under the Purchase Approval Matrix. Signature authority follows the same levels: department managers may sign contracts within their approval authority, Finance co-signs contracts above USD 10,000, and an Administrator co-signs contracts above USD 100,000. No employee may accept online terms or sign a contract outside this procedure.

### 3.5 Contract repository

All signed contracts must be uploaded to the contract repository within 5 business days of signature, with key data recorded: vendor, owner, value, start date, end date, renewal type, notice period and termination rights. Contracts are retained under the Procurement Records Retention policy.

### 3.6 Renewals

Contract owners must review auto-renewing contracts at least 90 days before the renewal date. The review confirms whether the service is still needed, whether pricing remains competitive and whether performance has been satisfactory. If the contract is not to be renewed, the contract owner sends the termination notice within the notice period stated in the contract. The contract repository sends reminders 120 days and 90 days before each renewal date. A renewal that increases the total value or crosses a new threshold in the Purchase Approval Matrix requires a new purchase request.

### 3.7 Performance and changes

Contract owners monitor vendor performance against agreed service levels and record significant issues. Changes to scope, price or term must be made through a written amendment approved under this procedure; informal changes agreed by email are not binding on Acme.

## 4. Responsibilities

- **Contract owners** manage the contract lifecycle, upload contracts, review renewals on time and track performance.
- **Department managers** approve contracts within their authority and assign contract owners.
- **Legal** reviews contracts, maintains templates and advises on disputes.
- **Finance** co-signs contracts above its threshold and checks payments against contract terms.
- **Administrators** co-sign the highest-value contracts.

## 5. Exceptions

Low-value purchases made under a purchase order with Acme's standard purchase terms do not need a separate contract. In an emergency under the Emergency Purchasing Procedure, a short-term agreement may be signed before Legal review, provided it runs for no more than 30 days and is reviewed by Legal within 10 business days. Any other exception requires written approval from Legal.

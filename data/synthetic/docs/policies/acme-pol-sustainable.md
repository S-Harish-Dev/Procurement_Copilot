# Sustainable Procurement Guidelines

**Effective date:** 2025-09-01
**Owning department:** Company-wide
**Classification:** PUBLIC

## 1. Purpose

Acme Corp recognises that the goods and services it buys have environmental and social effects well beyond its own operations. These guidelines explain how Acme considers sustainability when choosing vendors and products, so that purchasing decisions support lower emissions, less waste and fair working conditions while still delivering good value.

## 2. Scope

The guidelines apply to all purchases made by Acme in every department and to all vendors that supply Acme. They are used alongside the Acme Procurement Policy and the Supplier Code of Conduct. They are published externally so that current and prospective suppliers understand what Acme expects.

## 3. Guidelines

### 3.1 Sustainability target

Acme targets 40% of addressable spend with vendors holding a recognised sustainability certification by 2028. Addressable spend means third-party spend that Acme can influence through its sourcing decisions; it excludes taxes, payroll, government fees and payments to utilities where no alternative supplier exists. Recognised certifications include ISO 14001 environmental management certification, a published science-based emissions reduction target, B Corp certification and equivalent schemes accepted by the Procurement Operations team. Progress toward the target is reported annually.

### 3.2 Sustainability in vendor selection

For purchases that require at least three written quotes or a competitive tender under the Acme Procurement Policy, sustainability is part of the evaluation:

- For purchases above USD 25,000, quote requests ask vendors to state their certifications and environmental commitments.
- For competitive tenders above USD 250,000, sustainability carries a weighting of at least 10% of the total evaluation score.
- Where two offers are otherwise equivalent in price and quality, preference is given to the vendor with a recognised sustainability certification.

### 3.3 Product standards

When buying products, requesters should prefer:

- energy-efficient equipment carrying a recognised energy label;
- products with recycled content, repairable designs and take-back schemes;
- refillable or minimal packaging, with single-use plastic packaging avoided where alternatives exist;
- office paper certified from responsibly managed forests.

IT hardware is chosen with energy efficiency and repairability in mind, and retired devices are recycled through a certified recycler under the IT Asset Disposal Procedure.

### 3.4 Services and logistics

For services delivered on Acme premises, such as cleaning, catering and maintenance, vendors are asked to use low-impact materials and to minimise waste. For logistics and freight, Acme prefers consolidated shipments and lower-emission transport modes where delivery times allow.

### 3.5 Social responsibility

Acme expects suppliers to treat workers fairly and safely. Minimum standards, including the prohibition of forced and child labour, are set out in the Supplier Code of Conduct. Acme also welcomes offers from small businesses and diverse suppliers and makes sure tender requirements do not exclude them without good reason.

### 3.6 Vendor engagement

Acme reviews the sustainability performance of its largest vendors each year. Vendors without a recognised certification are encouraged to set a plan to obtain one within 24 months. Acme may share guidance and support with smaller vendors that are starting this process.

## 4. Responsibilities

- **Employees** consider sustainability when specifying needs and choosing products.
- **Department managers** support sustainable choices when approving purchase requests, including where a modest price premium is justified.
- **Finance** tracks certified spend against the target and reports progress.
- **The Procurement Operations team** includes sustainability criteria in quotes and tenders and maintains the list of recognised certifications.
- **Suppliers** provide accurate information about their certifications and environmental performance.

## 5. Exceptions

These guidelines do not replace the competitive sourcing and approval requirements in the Acme Procurement Policy and the Purchase Approval Matrix. Where no certified vendor can meet a requirement, or where a sustainable option would cost significantly more without a clear benefit, the requester records the reason in the purchase request. Emergency purchases are exempt from sustainability evaluation but are reviewed afterwards.

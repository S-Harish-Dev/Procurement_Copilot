# M&A Vendor Consolidation Plan (Project Larch)

**Effective date:** 2026-04-10
**Owning department:** Company-wide
**Classification:** RESTRICTED

## 1. Purpose

This plan sets out the objectives, timeline, governance and rules for Project Larch, Acme's programme to consolidate its logistics vendor base following the integration of recently acquired operations. It aims to reduce freight costs, simplify carrier management and lower operational and financial risk, while keeping service to customers uninterrupted during the transition.

## 2. Scope

Project Larch covers all logistics and freight vendors used by Acme and its acquired entities, including road haulage, parcel carriers, freight forwarders, customs brokers and third-party warehousing providers. Facilities services, IT and marketing vendors are outside its scope. Knowledge of the project is limited to the Project Larch steering group, nominated working-group members, Legal, Finance and Administrators.

## 3. Plan and Rules

### 3.1 Objectives

- Project Larch consolidates logistics vendors from 14 to 5 by end of FY2027.
- Reduce total logistics spend by at least 12% against the FY2025 baseline once consolidation is complete.
- Bring all remaining carriers onto Acme standard contract terms, including fuel surcharge bands tied to a published diesel index as required by Logistics and Freight Procurement.
- Reduce the number of High-tier logistics vendors on the vendor risk register to zero.

### 3.2 Phases and timeline

| Phase | Period | Main activities |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Baseline and design | Q2 FY2026 | Consolidate spend data, map lanes and volumes, define target carrier model. |
| 2. Competitive tender | Q3 to Q4 FY2026 | Run a competitive tender for consolidated lanes, as required for purchases above USD 250,000. |
| 3. Award and contracting | Q1 FY2027 | Select preferred carriers, negotiate contracts, complete Legal review. |
| 4. Transition | Q2 to Q3 FY2027 | Move volumes in waves, run parallel operations on critical lanes for at least 30 days. |
| 5. Exit and close | Q4 FY2027 | Terminate exiting vendors, confirm savings, close the programme. |

### 3.3 Interim purchasing rules

- From the effective date, no new logistics vendor may be onboarded without approval from the Project Larch steering group.
- Existing logistics contracts may not be renewed for more than 12 months, and any renewal must include termination for convenience on 90 days' notice or less.
- Purchase requests for logistics services continue to follow the Purchase Approval Matrix. Requests above USD 100,000 must also be reviewed by the steering group before submission.
- Existing vendors must not be told that they are being considered for exit before the communication plan in section 3.5 is approved.

### 3.4 Tender and evaluation

- Bids are evaluated on cost (50%), service performance and coverage (25%), financial and operational risk (15%) and sustainability credentials (10%).
- Every member of the evaluation panel must declare any interest in a bidding vendor before evaluation, in line with the Conflict of Interest Policy, and gifts from bidders above USD 100 in value may not be accepted while the tender is active.
- Contracts above USD 100,000 require Legal review before signature.

### 3.5 Communication and confidentiality

- The codename Project Larch must be used in all documents and meetings. Vendor names on the exit list are recorded only in the restricted programme workspace.
- Communication to exiting vendors is coordinated by Legal and Operations and issued in line with each contract's notice requirements.
- Information about the programme must not be shared with vendors, agencies or anyone outside the approved distribution.

### 3.6 Budget and benefits tracking

- The programme is funded from the Operations FY2026 budget, with a one-off transition allowance of USD 150,000 for parallel running and onboarding costs.
- Savings are validated by Finance using the method in Procurement Savings Targets FY2026 and reported to the steering group monthly.

## 4. Responsibilities

- **Steering group** (heads of Operations, Finance and Legal) sets direction, approves awards and resolves escalations.
- **Operations** leads the programme, manages carrier transitions and maintains service levels.
- **Finance** builds the spend baseline, runs tender commercials and validates savings.
- **Legal** drafts contracts, manages terminations and advises on confidentiality.
- **Administrators** restrict programme data to authorised users and apply vendor status changes.

## 5. Exceptions

Where an exiting vendor's failure would create a safety risk or a major outage, Operations may extend its contract or use an alternative carrier under the Emergency Purchasing Procedure, informing the steering group within 2 business days. Changes to the 14-to-5 target require steering group approval.

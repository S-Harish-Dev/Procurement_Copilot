# Purchasing Card (P-Card) Policy

**Effective date:** 2025-10-01
**Owning department:** Company-wide
**Classification:** INTERNAL

## 1. Purpose

Acme Corp issues purchasing cards (P-Cards) to selected employees so that low-value, one-time business purchases can be made quickly without the cost of a full purchase order and invoice cycle. This policy sets the limits, permitted uses and controls that apply to P-Cards.

## 2. Scope

The policy applies to every employee who holds a P-Card, to the department managers who approve their statements, and to Finance, which administers the programme. It applies in all departments. P-Cards are company property and may be used only for Acme business.

## 3. Rules

### 3.1 Eligibility and issue

A P-Card is issued at the request of a department manager to an employee who regularly makes low-value purchases. Cardholders must complete P-Card training and sign the cardholder agreement before the card is activated. Cards are issued in the cardholder's name and may not be shared or lent.

### 3.2 Spending limits

P-Card single transactions are limited to USD 2,500 and USD 10,000 per month per cardholder. Finance may set a lower limit for an individual cardholder at the department manager's request. A purchase may not be split into several transactions to stay within the single transaction limit; this is treated as threshold avoidance under the Acme Procurement Policy.

### 3.3 Approval of card purchases

P-Card purchases remain subject to the Purchase Approval Matrix. Purchases up to and including USD 1,000 may be made without prior approval. Purchases above USD 1,000, up to the USD 2,500 transaction limit, require department manager approval of a purchase request before the card is charged.

### 3.4 Permitted and prohibited uses

Permitted uses include office and workshop supplies, books, small tools, conference registration fees and one-time online purchases from approved vendors.

P-Cards may not be used for travel, gifts, or recurring subscriptions. In addition, P-Cards may not be used for:

- cash withdrawals, gift cards or other cash equivalents;
- computers, mobile devices or other IT hardware, which must be bought through IT;
- software or SaaS, which require an IT security review;
- personal purchases of any kind, even if repaid later;
- services from individuals or contractors who should be onboarded as vendors.

Travel is booked and claimed under the Travel and Expense Policy.

### 3.5 Receipts and reconciliation

Cardholders must keep an itemised receipt for every transaction. Each month, cardholders reconcile their statement in the expense system within 10 business days of the statement date, attaching receipts and a business purpose for each transaction. The department manager reviews and approves the reconciled statement within a further 5 business days.

### 3.6 Lost cards and disputes

Lost or stolen cards must be reported to the card issuer and Finance within 24 hours. Incorrect charges must be disputed with the issuer within 60 days of the statement date.

## 4. Responsibilities

- **Cardholders** use the card only for permitted purchases, stay within limits, keep receipts and reconcile on time.
- **Department managers** request cards, approve purchases above USD 1,000 and approve monthly statements.
- **Finance** issues and cancels cards, sets limits, audits transactions monthly and reports misuse.

## 5. Exceptions and Non-compliance

Temporary limit increases, for example for a one-off event, require written approval from the department manager and Finance and may last no longer than 30 days. Temporary increases apply to the monthly limit only; the USD 2,500 single transaction limit is never raised. Misuse of a P-Card leads to suspension of the card. Personal or unsupported charges must be repaid, and serious or repeated misuse may result in disciplinary action. Cards are cancelled when the cardholder leaves Acme or changes role.

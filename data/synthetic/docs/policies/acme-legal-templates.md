# Standard Contract Templates Guide

**Effective date:** 2025-11-20
**Owning department:** Legal
**Classification:** INTERNAL

## 1. Purpose

This guide explains which standard contract templates Acme uses for purchasing goods and services, when each template must be used, and which changes may be made without involving Legal. Using approved templates reduces negotiation time, keeps Acme's risk position consistent and allows most low-value contracts to be signed without a full Legal review.

## 2. Scope

This guide applies to all employees who prepare, negotiate or sign contracts with vendors on behalf of Acme. It covers purchasing and confidentiality agreements. Customer-facing sales contracts, employment contracts and real estate leases are outside its scope and must always be referred to Legal.

## 3. Templates and Rules for Use

### 3.1 Approved template library

The current versions of all templates are published in the Legal section of the contract repository. Only the latest version may be used; locally saved copies must not be reused.

| Template | Typical use |
|---|---|
| Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA) | Sharing confidential information with a prospective vendor before or during a sourcing exercise. |
| Master Services Agreement (MSA) | Ongoing services relationships; individual work is ordered through Statements of Work. |
| Statement of Work (SOW) | Defines scope, deliverables, milestones and fees under an MSA. |
| Goods Supply Agreement | Recurring purchases of physical goods, including stock items and equipment. |
| SaaS Subscription Addendum | Cloud software purchases, used together with the Software Licensing and SaaS Policy. |
| Data Processing Addendum (DPA) | Any vendor that processes personal data on Acme's behalf. |
| Purchase Order Terms and Conditions | Low-value, one-off purchases where no separate contract is signed. |

### 3.2 Choosing a template

- Purchases covered by a purchase order only, with no negotiated contract, are governed by the Purchase Order Terms and Conditions, which are attached automatically to every purchase order issued from the procurement system.
- A signed MSA or Goods Supply Agreement is required for any vendor relationship expected to exceed USD 25,000 in total value over 12 months.
- A mutual NDA must be signed before any confidential Acme information, including pricing, forecasts or technical specifications, is shared with a vendor.
- Vendors processing personal data must sign the DPA before access is granted, as required by the Vendor Data Protection Requirements.

### 3.3 Pre-approved fallback positions

Each template includes a fallback clause schedule listing alternative wording that business owners may accept without Legal review. Examples include:

- Payment terms between Net 30 and Net 60.
- A vendor liability cap of not less than 1x the annual contract value (the standard position is 2x).
- Termination for convenience on not less than 30 days' notice.
- Governing law of any US state in which Acme has an office.

Any change outside the fallback schedule, including edits to indemnities, intellectual property ownership, data protection, confidentiality or limitation of liability, must be reviewed by Legal.

### 3.4 Legal review requirements

- Contracts above USD 100,000 require Legal review before signature, even when the unmodified template is used.
- Any contract drafted on the vendor's paper rather than an Acme template requires Legal review regardless of value.
- Legal aims to return template-based reviews within 5 business days and vendor-paper reviews within 10 business days of receiving a complete request.

### 3.5 Signature authority

- Contracts may be signed only after the related purchase request has been approved under the Purchase Approval Matrix.
- The signatory must hold signature authority at or above the contract value and must not be the same person who approved the related purchase request at the final level.
- Electronic signature through Acme's approved e-signature platform is the default. Signed contracts must be uploaded to the contract repository within 5 business days of signature.

## 4. Responsibilities

- **Employees (contract owners)** select the correct template, keep negotiated changes within the fallback schedule and upload signed contracts.
- **Department managers** confirm the business need and approve purchase requests before contracts are signed.
- **Legal** maintains the templates and fallback schedules, reviews non-standard contracts and updates templates at least once a year.
- **Finance** checks that payment terms and pricing in signed contracts match the purchase order.

## 5. Exceptions

Where a vendor refuses to contract on Acme templates (for example, a large platform provider with non-negotiable terms), the contract owner must submit the vendor's terms to Legal with a short summary of the business need. Legal will document accepted deviations in the contract repository. No exception may be granted to the Legal review requirement for contracts above USD 100,000.

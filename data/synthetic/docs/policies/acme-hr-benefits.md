# Benefits Vendor Management

**Effective date:** 2025-11-15
**Owning department:** HR
**Classification:** INTERNAL

## 1. Purpose

This policy describes how Acme Corp selects, contracts, monitors and renews the vendors that deliver employee benefits. Benefits vendors handle sensitive employee data and directly affect employees' wellbeing, so they are managed with additional care beyond the standard procurement process.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to all vendors that provide or administer employee benefits, including:

- health, dental and vision insurance carriers and benefits brokers;
- retirement plan providers and administrators;
- life and disability insurance providers;
- employee assistance programme (EAP) providers;
- wellness, commuter and other voluntary benefit platforms.

It applies to HR, Finance, Legal and any employee involved in selecting or managing these vendors. Payroll providers are covered by the Acme Procurement Policy.

## 3. Rules

### 3.1 Selection

- Benefits vendors are selected and engaged only by HR. Other departments may not contract benefits vendors.
- Purchases must follow the Acme Procurement Policy: at least three written quotes are required for purchases above USD 25,000, and a competitive tender is required for purchases above USD 250,000. Quotes and tenders are evaluated on the total annual cost.
- Each benefits category is market-tested at least every 3 years, even if the current vendor is performing well.
- A benefits broker may be used to run market tests. Broker compensation, including any commission received from carriers, must be disclosed in writing to HR before the market test starts.

### 3.2 Due diligence

- New benefits vendors follow the Vendor Onboarding Procedure, including the risk review.
- Benefits vendors process employee personal data and, often, health data. They must sign the Data Processing Addendum before access is granted and must notify Acme of a personal data breach within 72 hours.
- Any benefits platform that employees log into must pass an IT security review and support SSO.
- Vendors must hold the licences and regulatory registrations required to provide the benefit in each country where Acme employees are covered.

### 3.3 Contracts

- Benefits contracts use Legal's standard templates where possible. Contracts above USD 100,000 require Legal review before signature.
- Contract terms are normally 1 to 3 years. Contracts must include service levels, data return and deletion terms, and the right for Acme to audit.
- Contract owners must review auto-renewing contracts at least 90 days before the renewal date. For benefits plans, HR starts the renewal review at least 120 days before the plan year so that changes can be communicated before open enrolment.

### 3.4 Approvals

Benefits purchases follow the Purchase Approval Matrix based on the total annual contract value. The HR department manager approves at manager level; requests above USD 10,000 additionally require Finance approval, and requests above USD 100,000 additionally require approval by an Administrator.

### 3.5 Service monitoring

HR holds a service review with each major benefits vendor at least twice a year. Reviews cover:

- claims processing: at least 95% of claims processed within 10 business days;
- employee enquiries: at least 90% answered within 2 business days;
- complaints, escalations and their resolution;
- premium and fee changes, and invoice accuracy.

Vendors that miss service levels for two consecutive reviews are placed on a written improvement plan of no more than 90 days.

### 3.6 Invoices and premiums

Premium and fee invoices are reconciled by HR against enrolment data before they are sent to Finance for payment. Discrepancies above USD 500 are raised with the vendor before payment.

## 4. Responsibilities

- **Employees** report service issues with a benefits vendor to HR.
- **Department managers** in HR approve benefits purchase requests and own the vendor relationships.
- **HR** owns this policy, runs market tests, manages contracts, reconciles invoices and conducts service reviews.
- **Finance** approves higher-value requests, pays premiums and reviews benefits cost against budget.
- **Legal** reviews contracts and regulatory requirements.
- **Administrators** approve requests above USD 100,000.

## 5. Exceptions

Where a benefit is required by law or a collective agreement and only one provider is available, HR may engage that provider without competitive quotes, with written approval from Finance. The reason is recorded on the purchase request.

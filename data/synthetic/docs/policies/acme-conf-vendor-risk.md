# Vendor Risk Register Summary

**Effective date:** 2026-03-15
**Owning department:** Finance
**Classification:** CONFIDENTIAL

## 1. Purpose

This document summarises how Acme maintains its vendor risk register, how vendors are scored and tiered, and the current position of the register as at the effective date. It gives Finance, Legal and department managers a consistent basis for deciding which vendors need closer monitoring, contingency plans or remediation.

## 2. Scope

The register covers every active vendor in the procurement system with annual spend above USD 10,000, every vendor that processes personal data on Acme's behalf regardless of spend, and every vendor classified as critical to operations. Vendors below these levels are risk reviewed at onboarding only.

## 3. Register Rules and Current Position

### 3.1 Risk dimensions

Each vendor is scored from 1 (low) to 5 (high) on six dimensions:

| Dimension | What is assessed |
|---|---|
| Financial | Credit rating, payment history, dependence on Acme revenue. |
| Operational | Single-source exposure, delivery performance, business continuity plans. |
| Information security | Results of the IT vendor security review, SOC 2 Type II status, access to Acme systems. |
| Data protection | Volume and sensitivity of personal data processed, signed Data Processing Addendum. |
| Compliance and ethics | Sanctions screening, anti-bribery controls, adherence to the Supplier Code of Conduct. |
| Contractual | Contract in place, liability caps, termination rights, auto-renewal exposure. |

### 3.2 Risk tiers

- The overall score is the average of the six dimension scores, except that any single dimension scored 5 places the vendor in the High tier automatically.
- **High:** overall score of 3.5 or above. High-risk vendors require Legal sign-off at onboarding and a documented mitigation plan.
- **Medium:** overall score from 2.5 to below 3.5.
- **Low:** overall score below 2.5.

### 3.3 Review frequency

- High-tier vendors are reassessed every 6 months, Medium-tier vendors every 12 months and Low-tier vendors every 24 months.
- An immediate reassessment is triggered by a data breach notification, a missed delivery that causes an outage, a credit downgrade, a change of ownership, or a request to change bank details.
- Vendors that fail to supply requested information within 15 business days are moved up one tier until the information is received.

### 3.4 Actions by tier

- High-tier vendors may not receive new purchase orders above USD 25,000 unless the mitigation plan is current and has been reviewed by Finance in the last 6 months.
- Where a High-tier vendor's risk cannot be reduced, Finance may recommend suspension. Suspended vendors may not receive new orders.
- Critical single-source vendors in any tier must have a documented contingency plan identifying at least one alternative supplier.

### 3.5 Register position as at 15 March 2026

| Tier | Vendors | Share of annual spend |
|---|---|---|
| High | 9 | 14% |
| Medium | 41 | 37% |
| Low | 118 | 49% |
| **Total** | **168** | **100%** |

Key observations from the Q1 2026 review:

- Four of the nine High-tier vendors are logistics and freight carriers, reflecting thin margins and fuel cost exposure. These vendors are included in the logistics consolidation programme overseen by Operations.
- Two High-tier vendors are SaaS providers whose SOC 2 Type II reports have lapsed. IT has requested updated reports, and renewals are on hold until they are received.
- One facilities services vendor was moved to High after a credit downgrade. Operations has identified an alternative supplier, and the existing contract's 30-day termination for convenience clause gives Acme a fallback.
- Two High-tier vendors are marketing agencies with significant single-source exposure for ongoing campaigns.
- Three vendors were suspended in the period, all for failing sanctions or documentation checks.

## 4. Responsibilities

- **Finance** owns the register, coordinates scoring, reports quarterly to leadership and recommends suspensions.
- **Legal** signs off High-tier vendors, reviews contractual mitigations and advises on termination options.
- **IT** provides the information security score through the IT vendor security review.
- **Department managers** act as relationship owners, provide operational performance data and maintain contingency plans for critical vendors.
- **Administrators** apply vendor status changes, including suspensions, in the procurement system.

## 5. Exceptions

A department manager may request that a High-tier vendor receive a new order above USD 25,000 without a current mitigation plan only where no alternative supplier exists and the purchase is needed to prevent a safety risk or major outage. Such requests require written approval from Finance and Legal and must be revisited within 30 days.

## 6. Confidentiality

Vendor scores and tier assignments are confidential. They must not be disclosed to vendors or used in communications outside Finance, Legal, the relationship owner and Administrators.

# Month-End Close: Accruals for Purchases

**Effective date:** 2025-10-20
**Owning department:** Finance
**Classification:** INTERNAL

## 1. Purpose

This procedure describes how Acme records purchasing costs that have been incurred but not yet invoiced at month end. Accurate accruals make sure that each month's results reflect the goods and services actually received, that department budget reports are reliable, and that Acme's financial statements comply with accrual accounting requirements.

## 2. Scope

This procedure applies to all purchases made through the procurement system, including goods, services, subscriptions and blanket purchase order releases, across all departments. It also covers emergency purchases made before a purchase request was approved. Payroll, tax and intercompany accruals are handled under separate Finance procedures.

## 3. Accrual Rules

### 3.1 Close calendar

- The month-end close runs over the first 5 business days of the following month.
- Business day 1: receiving deadline. All goods and services received in the prior month must be receipted in the procurement system by the end of business day 1.
- Business day 2: departments submit service accrual confirmations.
- Business day 3: Finance runs the automated goods accrual and posts manual accruals.
- Business days 4 and 5: review, variance analysis and reporting to department managers.

### 3.2 Goods received not invoiced

- Goods received but not invoiced by the 3rd business day are accrued at the PO price.
- The accrual uses the quantity receipted in the procurement system multiplied by the unit price on the purchase order, excluding sales tax unless the tax is non-recoverable.
- Freight and other charges are accrued only when they are listed as separate lines on the purchase order.
- Where goods have been physically received but not receipted in the system, the receiving employee must complete the receipt before the business day 1 deadline. Late receipts are accrued in the following month.

### 3.3 Services received not invoiced

- For services, the purchase order owner must confirm by business day 2 the value of work performed in the month and not yet invoiced.
- Time-based services are accrued on hours worked multiplied by the contracted rate. Milestone-based services are accrued only when the milestone has been accepted.
- Outside counsel matters are accrued based on the monthly work-in-progress estimate provided by the law firm and confirmed by Legal.
- Service accruals below USD 1,000 per purchase order are not required unless the department manager requests them.

### 3.4 Prepayments and subscriptions

- Annual or multi-month subscriptions paid in advance are recorded as prepayments and expensed evenly over the service period.
- Prepayments above USD 5,000 must be supported by the contract or invoice showing the service period.

### 3.5 Reversal and matching

- All month-end accruals are reversed automatically on the first day of the following month.
- When the vendor invoice arrives, it is processed through the three-way match described in Goods Receipt and Three-Way Match. Any difference between the accrued amount and the final invoice is recognised in the month the invoice is posted.
- Accruals older than 90 days with no matching invoice are reviewed by Finance with the purchase order owner and either confirmed, adjusted or released.

### 3.6 Budget reporting

- Accrued amounts count as actual spend against department budgets in the month they are accrued.
- Department managers receive a budget-versus-actual report, including accruals, by business day 5.

## 4. Responsibilities

| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| Employee (requester or receiver) | Receipts goods and services in the procurement system by business day 1. |
| Department manager | Confirms service accruals by business day 2 and reviews the monthly budget report. |
| Finance | Runs the accrual process, posts manual accruals and reversals, reviews aged accruals and issues budget reports. |
| Administrator | Maintains the scheduled accrual job and close calendar settings in the procurement system. |

## 5. Exceptions

- Where the purchase order price is known to be wrong (for example, an agreed price change not yet reflected on the PO), Finance may accrue at the corrected price if written evidence is attached.
- Single accruals above USD 100,000 require review by the Finance department manager before posting.
- Any other deviation from this procedure must be approved by Finance and documented in the close file.

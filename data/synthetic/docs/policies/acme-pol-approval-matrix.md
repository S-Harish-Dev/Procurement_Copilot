# Purchase Approval Matrix

**Effective date:** 2026-01-01
**Owning department:** Company-wide
**Classification:** INTERNAL

## 1. Purpose

This document defines who must approve a purchase request at Acme Corp and in what order. It provides a single, consistent approval structure so that spending is authorized at a level proportionate to its value and risk, and so that no individual can commit Acme to significant expenditure alone.

## 2. Scope

The matrix applies to every purchase request raised in the procurement system by any employee in any department, including requests for goods, services, software, hardware and contract renewals. It applies to the total value of the request in USD, including taxes, delivery and the full committed term of recurring charges.

## 3. Approval Levels

### 3.1 Thresholds

Approval levels are cumulative. Each higher level is added to the levels below it; it does not replace them.

| Request amount (USD) | Required approvals |
|---|---|
| 0 to 1,000 (inclusive) | Automatic approval |
| Above 1,000 up to 10,000 | Department manager |
| Above 10,000 up to 100,000 | Department manager, then Finance |
| Above 100,000 | Department manager, then Finance, then Administrator |

- Purchase requests up to and including USD 1,000 are approved automatically.
- Requests above USD 1,000 require approval by the requester's department manager.
- Requests above USD 10,000 additionally require Finance approval.
- Requests above USD 100,000 additionally require approval by an Administrator.

For example, a request for USD 1,000 is approved automatically, a request for USD 1,001 needs the department manager, and a request for USD 150,000 needs the department manager, Finance and an Administrator, in that order.

### 3.2 Sequence

Approvals are collected in order, starting at the lowest required level. A request moves to the next level only after the current level has approved it. A rejection at any level ends the workflow; the requester may revise and resubmit the request, which then restarts at the first level.

### 3.3 Separation of duties

No one may approve their own request, and one person may approve only one level of a request. If the requester is also the department manager, the manager-level approval is performed by Finance. If a person holds both a Finance role and an Administrator role, they may approve at one of those levels only, and the other level must be completed by a different person.

### 3.4 Personal approval limits

Finance may assign an individual department manager a personal approval limit lower than the standard manager level. If a manager's personal approval limit is lower than the amount and the manager is the final level, the request is routed to Finance. Where Finance approval is already required by the thresholds in section 3.1, the manager approves within the normal sequence and Finance completes its own level as usual.

### 3.5 Unavailable managers

Departments without an available manager have their manager-level approvals handled by Finance. This applies when a manager position is vacant or when the manager is on extended leave without a recorded delegate. Finance must not then also approve the Finance level of the same request; a second Finance approver is required.

### 3.6 Delegation

Department managers may delegate their approval authority for a defined period of up to 30 days to another manager in the procurement system. A delegate approves under the same thresholds and separation-of-duties rules as the original approver.

## 4. Responsibilities

- **Employees** submit complete purchase requests and state the full value. Splitting a purchase to stay under a threshold is prohibited under the Acme Procurement Policy.
- **Department managers** confirm business need and budget before approving and act on pending approvals within 3 business days.
- **Finance** reviews budget availability, commercial terms and compliance with sourcing rules, and handles routed and unassigned manager-level approvals.
- **Administrators** approve the highest-value requests and maintain the approval configuration in the procurement system.

## 5. Exceptions

There are no exceptions to the separation-of-duties rules in section 3.3. Purchases made under the Emergency Purchasing Procedure must still be approved retrospectively at the levels required by this matrix. Changes to the thresholds in this document may be made only through a formal revision approved by Finance and an Administrator.

# Travel and Expense Policy

**Effective date:** 2026-01-01
**Owning department:** Company-wide
**Classification:** INTERNAL

## 1. Purpose

This policy sets the standards for booking business travel and claiming business expenses at Acme Corp. It aims to keep employees safe and reasonably comfortable while travelling, control travel costs and make sure every reimbursed expense is genuine and properly documented.

## 2. Scope

The policy applies to all employees and contractors who travel or incur expenses on Acme business, in every department. It covers air and rail travel, hotels, ground transport, meals and incidental expenses. Purchasing cards may not be used for travel; see the Purchasing Card (P-Card) Policy.

## 3. Rules

### 3.1 Pre-approval

All overnight trips and all international trips must be approved by the employee's department manager before booking. Trips with an estimated total cost above USD 5,000 must also be noted to Finance for budget tracking. Travel must be booked through Acme's corporate travel booking tool unless the tool cannot offer the required itinerary.

### 3.2 Air travel

Economy class is required for flights under 6 hours; premium economy is allowed for 6 hours or more. Flight duration means scheduled time in the air for a single flight segment, excluding layovers. Business class is not permitted except under section 5. Employees should book at least 14 days in advance where possible and choose the lowest logical fare that fits the business schedule.

### 3.3 Rail and ground transport

Standard class rail is required for journeys under 3 hours. Taxis and ride-share services may be used where public transport is impractical. Car rental is limited to compact or intermediate cars. Personal car use is reimbursed at the published Acme mileage rate.

### 3.4 Accommodation

The hotel cap is USD 250 per night in tier-1 cities and USD 180 elsewhere. The cap covers the room rate excluding taxes. Tier-1 cities are listed in the travel booking tool and are reviewed by Finance each January. Employees should use Acme's preferred hotels where available.

### 3.5 Meals

The meal per diem is USD 75 per day. It covers breakfast, lunch, dinner and non-alcoholic drinks. On partial travel days (the day of departure and the day of return), the per diem is reduced to USD 40. Meals already provided by a hotel, conference or client are not claimed. Alcohol is not reimbursed except as part of approved client hospitality.

### 3.6 Non-reimbursable items

The following are not reimbursed: personal entertainment, minibar charges, fines and penalties, upgrades paid at the employee's choice, travel insurance (Acme provides cover), and costs for accompanying family members.

### 3.7 Expense claims

Expense claims must be submitted within 30 days with itemised receipts. Card payment slips alone are not accepted as receipts. Claims are submitted in the expense system and approved by the department manager. Claims submitted after 30 days require additional Finance approval and may be refused. Approved claims are reimbursed in the next scheduled payment run.

## 4. Responsibilities

- **Employees** plan travel economically, book through the approved channel, keep itemised receipts and submit accurate claims on time.
- **Department managers** pre-approve trips, check claims against this policy and approve them within 5 business days.
- **Finance** audits a sample of claims each month, maintains the tier-1 city list and processes reimbursements.

## 5. Exceptions

Exceptions, such as business class for a medical reason or a hotel above the cap during a major event, require written approval from the department manager and Finance before booking. The approval must be attached to the expense claim. Expenses claimed in breach of this policy are not reimbursed, and false claims may result in disciplinary action.

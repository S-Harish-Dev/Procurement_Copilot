# Goods Receipt and Three-Way Match

**Effective date:** 2025-11-01
**Owning department:** Company-wide
**Classification:** INTERNAL

## 1. Purpose

This procedure ensures that Acme Corp pays only for goods and services that were properly ordered, actually received and correctly invoiced. It describes how receipts are recorded, how invoices are matched and how discrepancies are resolved before payment.

## 2. Scope

The procedure applies to all vendor invoices relating to purchase orders, for goods and services, in all departments. Purchasing card transactions and employee expense claims are outside its scope and are covered by the Purchasing Card (P-Card) Policy and the Travel and Expense Policy.

## 3. Procedure

### 3.1 Goods receipt

The requester, or the Operations receiving team for deliveries to Acme warehouses and sites, records a goods receipt in the procurement system within 3 business days of delivery. The receipt states the quantity received and confirms that the goods match the order and are in acceptable condition. For services, the requester records a service receipt once the work, or an agreed milestone, has been completed to a satisfactory standard.

Damaged, incorrect or incomplete deliveries must be recorded as such and reported to the vendor within 5 business days. Only accepted quantities are receipted.

### 3.2 Three-way match

Invoices are paid only after a three-way match of purchase order, goods receipt and invoice. The procurement system compares:

- the vendor, item, quantity and unit price on the purchase order;
- the quantity recorded on the goods receipt;
- the quantity, unit price and total on the invoice.

An invoice that matches within tolerance is released for payment in the next payment run. Invoices must reference a valid purchase order number; invoices without one are returned to the vendor.

### 3.3 Variances

Price variances above 5% or USD 500 require buyer approval before payment. A variance that exceeds either limit triggers a hold, so a USD 600 difference on a USD 20,000 invoice requires approval even though it is only 3%. The buyer, normally the requester who raised the purchase request, either approves the variance with a reason or asks the vendor for a corrected invoice.

Quantity variances, where more is invoiced than was received, are never paid automatically. Finance holds the invoice until the goods receipt is updated or the vendor issues a credit note.

If an approved variance increases the total purchase value above a threshold in the Purchase Approval Matrix, the purchase request must be amended and re-approved at the new level before payment.

### 3.4 Payment

Matched invoices are paid according to the vendor's payment terms in Finance's scheduled payment runs. Invoices on hold for more than 30 days are escalated by Finance to the department manager of the requester.

### 3.5 Duplicate and suspicious invoices

The procurement system checks every invoice for duplicates based on vendor, invoice number, date and amount. Possible duplicates and invoices showing bank details different from the vendor master are held and referred to Finance for review. Bank detail changes are only made under the Vendor Onboarding Procedure.

## 4. Responsibilities

- **Requesters (buyers)** record goods and service receipts promptly and review variances on their purchase orders.
- **The Operations receiving team** inspects and receipts deliveries to Acme warehouses and sites.
- **Department managers** resolve escalated invoice holds for their department.
- **Finance** operates the matching process, manages holds and processes payments.

## 5. Exceptions

Some recurring charges, such as utilities and approved subscriptions billed against a blanket purchase order, may be paid on a two-way match of purchase order and invoice where Finance has approved this in advance for the specific vendor. Payments without a purchase order are permitted only for items Finance has designated as non-PO spend, such as taxes and government fees. Any other exception requires written approval from Finance.

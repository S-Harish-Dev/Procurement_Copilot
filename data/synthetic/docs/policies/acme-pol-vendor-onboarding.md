# Vendor Onboarding Procedure

**Effective date:** 2026-02-01
**Owning department:** Company-wide
**Classification:** INTERNAL

## 1. Purpose

This procedure describes how a new vendor is added to Acme Corp's vendor master so that it can receive purchase orders and payments. It protects Acme against payment fraud, sanctions exposure, uninsured losses and vendors that cannot meet Acme's standards.

## 2. Scope

The procedure applies to every new supplier of goods or services, including one-time vendors, software providers, consultants and agencies, in all departments. It also covers changes to the details of existing vendors, in particular bank details. Under the Acme Procurement Policy, no purchase order may be issued to a vendor that has not completed onboarding.

## 3. Procedure

### 3.1 Requesting a new vendor

An employee who needs a new vendor submits a vendor request in the procurement system, stating the goods or services to be supplied, the expected annual spend and the business reason for not using an existing approved vendor. The department manager must endorse the vendor request before it is sent to Finance.

### 3.2 Required documents

New vendors must provide a W-9 or equivalent tax form, a bank confirmation letter and an insurance certificate. In addition, vendors must:

- accept the Supplier Code of Conduct;
- complete the vendor information form (legal name, registered address and ownership);
- where they will process personal data, sign the Data Processing Addendum described in the Vendor Data Protection Requirements.

The bank confirmation letter must be issued by the vendor's bank on bank letterhead and dated within the last 90 days. The insurance certificate must show general liability cover valid for at least the next 6 months.

### 3.3 Timeline

Onboarding takes 10 business days after all documents are received. The clock does not start until the document set is complete. Requesters should allow for this lead time when planning purchases; incomplete submissions are returned to the vendor and the clock restarts.

### 3.4 Risk review

Every new vendor undergoes a risk review; high-risk vendors require Legal sign-off. Finance rates each vendor as low, medium or high risk based on:

- expected annual spend (vendors expected to exceed USD 100,000 per year are at least medium risk);
- access to Acme systems, facilities or personal data;
- country of operation and sanctions screening results;
- financial stability and adverse media checks.

Vendors with access to Acme systems or data also complete the IT security review required by the Software Licensing and SaaS Policy before activation.

### 3.5 Activation

Once documents are verified and the risk review is complete, Finance sets the vendor to active in the vendor master. Only active vendors may receive new orders. A vendor that fails the risk review, or does not supply missing documents within 30 days of request, is closed and must restart onboarding.

### 3.6 Bank detail changes

Bank detail changes require written notice and a telephone call-back to a known contact. The call-back must use a telephone number already held in the vendor master or on a previously validated contract, never a number supplied in the change request itself. Finance places a payment hold on the vendor until the change has been verified, and a second Finance team member must confirm the change before it is saved.

### 3.7 Periodic review

Finance re-verifies the documents of active vendors every 24 months. Vendors that have had no purchase orders for 18 months are set to inactive and must be reactivated through this procedure.

## 4. Responsibilities

- **Employees** raise vendor requests, explain the business need and communicate document requirements to the vendor.
- **Department managers** endorse vendor requests and confirm that an existing vendor cannot meet the need.
- **Finance** verifies documents, performs risk reviews, maintains the vendor master and processes bank detail changes.
- **Legal** signs off high-risk vendors and advises on contract terms.
- **IT** performs security reviews for vendors with system or data access.

## 5. Exceptions

For a genuine emergency under the Emergency Purchasing Procedure, Finance may approve a temporary vendor activation for a single order once the tax form and bank confirmation letter have been verified. The remaining documents must be received within 10 business days or the vendor is suspended. There are no exceptions to the bank detail call-back requirement.

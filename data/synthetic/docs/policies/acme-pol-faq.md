# Procurement FAQ for Employees

**Effective date:** 2026-04-01
**Owning department:** Company-wide
**Classification:** PUBLIC

## 1. Purpose

This FAQ answers the questions employees most often ask about buying goods and services at Acme Corp. It summarises existing policies in plain language. If this FAQ and a policy ever differ, the policy applies.

## 2. Scope

This FAQ is for all Acme employees in every department. It covers purchase requests, approvals, quotes, vendors, P-Cards, IT purchases, emergencies and invoices. It does not cover payroll, travel bookings or sales contracts with customers.

## 3. Buying Goods and Services

### 3.1 How do I buy something for my team?

Raise a purchase request in the procurement system. All purchases of goods and services must be raised as a purchase request in the procurement system, unless the purchase is a permitted P-Card transaction. Attach quotes and a short business justification.

### 3.2 Who approves my request?

Approval depends on the total value of the request:

| Request value | Approval required |
|---|---|
| Up to and including USD 1,000 | Approved automatically |
| Above USD 1,000 | Department manager |
| Above USD 10,000 | Department manager and Finance |
| Above USD 100,000 | Department manager, Finance and an Administrator |

No one may approve their own request, and one person may approve only one level of a request. The Purchase Approval Matrix has the full rules.

### 3.3 How many quotes do I need?

At least three written quotes are required for purchases above USD 25,000. A competitive tender, run with Finance, is required for purchases above USD 250,000. For smaller purchases, one quote is enough, but comparing prices is encouraged.

### 3.4 Can I split an order to make approval faster?

No. Splitting a purchase into smaller requests to avoid an approval threshold is prohibited and may lead to disciplinary action.

## 4. Vendors

### 4.1 Can I use any supplier?

Purchases must use an approved (active) vendor. Suspended vendors may not receive new orders. Search the vendor list in the procurement system before contacting a supplier.

### 4.2 How do I add a new vendor?

Submit a vendor onboarding request. The vendor must provide a tax form, a bank confirmation letter and an insurance certificate. Onboarding takes 10 business days after all documents are received, so plan ahead.

### 4.3 A vendor offered me a gift. What should I do?

Gifts from vendors above USD 100 in value must be declared and may not be accepted during an active tender. See the Conflict of Interest Policy.

## 5. Cards, IT and Software

### 5.1 When can I use a P-Card?

P-Cards are for small, one-off purchases. Single transactions are limited to USD 2,500, and spend is limited to USD 10,000 per month per cardholder. P-Cards may not be used for travel, gifts or recurring subscriptions.

### 5.2 Can I buy my own laptop or monitor and claim it back?

No. All hardware must be purchased through IT; personal purchases are not reimbursed. IT replaces standard laptops on a fixed refresh cycle.

### 5.3 Can I sign up for a new online tool?

Not without review. All SaaS subscriptions require an IT security review before purchase, even if the tool is free or low cost.

## 6. Emergencies, Invoices and Payments

### 6.1 What if something must be bought immediately?

Emergency purchases are allowed before approval only to prevent safety risks or major outages. A retrospective purchase request must then be raised within 2 business days.

### 6.2 When does a vendor get paid?

Invoices are paid only after the purchase order, goods receipt and invoice match. Record receipt of goods or services in the procurement system promptly so that payment is not delayed. Payment runs take place twice a week.

### 6.3 How long are my purchase records kept?

Procurement records are retained for 7 years after contract end. Keep all supporting documents in the procurement system, not on personal drives.

## 7. Responsibilities

- **Employees** raise accurate requests, use approved vendors and record receipts.
- **Department managers** review requests for business need and budget.
- **Finance** approves higher-value requests, manages vendors and processes payments.
- **Administrators** approve the highest-value requests and manage system access.

## 8. Exceptions and Help

Any exception to a procurement rule must be requested in writing through the procurement system and approved under the relevant policy before the purchase is made. For questions not covered here, contact the Finance procurement team through the procurement system help page. Concerns about misconduct can be raised confidentially through the ethics hotline.

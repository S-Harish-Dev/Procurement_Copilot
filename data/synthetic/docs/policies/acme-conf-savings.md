# Procurement Savings Targets FY2026

**Effective date:** 2026-01-08
**Owning department:** Finance
**Classification:** CONFIDENTIAL

## 1. Purpose

This document sets Acme's procurement savings target for FY2026, explains how savings are defined and measured, and assigns responsibility for delivering and reporting them. Consistent measurement makes sure that savings reported by departments are real, comparable and reflected in budgets.

## 2. Scope

The target applies to all addressable spend, meaning third-party spend on goods and services that Acme can influence through sourcing, negotiation or demand management. Addressable spend excludes taxes, payroll, statutory fees, intercompany charges, charitable donations and payments fixed by regulation. All departments with a FY2026 purchasing budget are in scope.

## 3. Targets and Measurement Rules

### 3.1 Company target

- The FY2026 procurement savings target is 6% of addressable spend.
- Based on planned addressable spend of USD 6,800,000 across department budgets, the target equals approximately USD 408,000. The final target amount is recalculated after the mid-year review using actual addressable spend.

### 3.2 Department contributions

| Department | Contribution target | Main savings levers |
|---|---|---|
| IT | 7% | SaaS licence rationalisation, reserved instances and savings plans for cloud workloads, hardware bundle pricing. |
| Operations | 8% | Logistics vendor consolidation, freight rate renegotiation, facilities contract retendering. |
| Marketing | 5% | Agency retainer review against KPIs, event package negotiations. |
| Legal | 4% | Fixed-fee arrangements and stricter outside counsel billing review. |
| HR | 3% | Recruitment agency fee renegotiation, consolidated training providers. |
| Finance | 3% | Banking fee renegotiation, audit scope optimisation. |

Department contributions are weighted so that, at planned spend levels, they deliver at least the 6% company target, with a small contingency for categories that fall short.

### 3.3 What counts as a saving

- **Price reduction:** a lower unit price than the previous contract price for the same goods or services, measured over the first 12 months of the new contract.
- **Cost avoidance:** a vendor's proposed price increase that is reduced or rejected. Cost avoidance is reported separately and counts towards no more than one third of a department's target.
- **Demand reduction:** a documented reduction in volume, such as cancelled unused licences or consolidated shipments.
- **Payment term improvement:** does not count as a saving. Improved terms are tracked separately as a working capital benefit.

### 3.4 Baselines and validation

- The baseline is the price paid in the previous 12 months. For new categories with no history, the baseline is the average of the compliant quotes received, excluding the lowest.
- Every saving above USD 10,000 must be validated by Finance before it is reported, with supporting evidence such as the previous contract, quotes and the new agreement.
- Savings are recognised only once the related contract is signed and the purchase order is issued in the procurement system.
- One-off savings are counted once. Recurring savings are counted for the first 12 months only.

### 3.5 Reporting

- Departments submit savings to Finance by the 10th business day after each quarter end.
- Finance publishes a quarterly savings report to department managers and Administrators, showing validated savings against target.
- Validated savings are reflected in department budgets at the mid-year review.

## 4. Responsibilities

- **Department managers** own their department's contribution target, identify opportunities and submit savings with evidence.
- **Finance** sets baselines, validates and reports savings and adjusts budgets.
- **Legal** supports contract renegotiation and makes sure new terms do not increase Acme's risk.
- **Employees** raising purchase requests follow the quote and tender rules in the Acme Procurement Policy, which are a key source of savings.

## 5. Exceptions

Savings achieved through actions that breach Acme policies, such as splitting purchases to avoid approval thresholds, waiving required quotes or using suspended vendors, will not be recognised. Where a saving would conflict with the Sustainable Procurement Guidelines or reduce quality or safety, the department manager must refer the decision to Finance before proceeding.

## 6. Confidentiality

Savings targets and contribution percentages are confidential and must not be disclosed to vendors.

# FY2026 Budget Allocation Memo

**Effective date:** 2025-12-20
**Owning department:** Finance
**Classification:** CONFIDENTIAL

## 1. Purpose

This memo records the approved FY2026 operating budget allocations for purchased goods and services by department, and sets the rules for how those budgets are monitored, committed and reallocated during the year. It is the reference document for budget checks performed when purchase requests are approved.

## 2. Scope

This memo applies to all non-payroll operating expenditure that is purchased through the procurement system, including goods, services, software subscriptions, cloud spend, outside counsel and agency fees. Capital projects approved through the separate capital planning process, payroll and employee benefits costs are not included in these figures. Distribution is limited to Finance, Legal, department managers and Administrators.

## 3. Budget Allocations and Rules

### 3.1 Approved FY2026 allocations

FY2026 budgets: IT USD 2,500,000; Operations USD 1,800,000; Marketing USD 1,200,000; Legal USD 600,000; HR USD 400,000; Finance USD 300,000.

### 3.2 Basis of allocation

- IT's allocation reflects the laptop refresh cycle under the IT Hardware Standards, growth in SaaS subscriptions, and cloud infrastructure spend governed by the IT Cloud Spend Guidelines.
- Operations' allocation covers facilities services, logistics and freight, and inventory replenishment. It assumes early savings from the logistics vendor consolidation programme.
- Marketing's allocation includes agency retainers and the events and sponsorship programme.
- Legal's allocation is driven mainly by outside counsel fees for ongoing matters.
- HR's allocation covers recruitment agency fees, training purchases and benefits vendor administration. Individual training allowances are funded from this budget.
- Finance's allocation covers audit support, banking services and finance systems.

### 3.3 Budget control

- Approved purchase requests commit budget at the time of approval. Actual spend, including month-end accruals, is recorded against the budget when goods or services are received.
- A purchase request that would take a department above its remaining annual budget may not be approved until Finance confirms that funding is available or a reallocation has been agreed.
- Budgets are phased by quarter. Departments may not commit more than 60% of their annual budget before 30 June without Finance approval.
- Department managers receive a monthly budget-versus-actual report by the 5th business day of each month.

### 3.4 Mid-year review and reallocation

- A mid-year review in July may reallocate up to 10% of any department's unspent budget.
- Reallocation decisions are proposed by Finance and agreed with the affected department managers. Funds are moved only from unspent, uncommitted balances as at 30 June.
- Requests for additional funding outside the mid-year review require a written business case approved by Finance and an Administrator.
- Unspent budget does not carry forward into FY2027.

### 3.5 Savings and targets

The allocations above already include the expected effect of the FY2026 procurement savings programme. Savings achieved above plan are retained centrally by Finance and are considered for redistribution at the mid-year review.

## 4. Responsibilities

- **Department managers** manage spend within their allocation, review the monthly report and explain variances above 5% of the quarterly phasing.
- **Finance** maintains budget figures in the procurement system, performs budget checks, runs the mid-year review and reports to leadership.
- **Administrators** configure budget values and controls in the procurement system and approve out-of-cycle funding requests together with Finance.
- **Employees** check that a budget line exists before raising a purchase request.

## 5. Exceptions

Spend required under the Emergency Purchasing Procedure may temporarily exceed a department's remaining budget. Finance will review such cases at the next month-end close and agree with the department manager how the overspend is to be recovered. No other overspend is permitted without written Finance approval.

## 6. Confidentiality

This memo contains confidential financial planning information. It must not be shared with vendors, agencies or any external party, and budget figures must not be quoted in negotiations.

# IT Hardware Standards

**Effective date:** 2026-03-01
**Owning department:** Company-wide
**Classification:** INTERNAL

## 1. Purpose

These standards define the computer hardware Acme Corp provides to employees, how often it is replaced and how it is bought. Standard configurations reduce support costs, keep devices secure and manageable, and allow IT to negotiate better prices with hardware vendors.

## 2. Scope

The standards apply to all laptops, workstations, monitors, docking stations, mobile phones, tablets and peripherals used for Acme business by employees and contractors in every department. Servers, network equipment and cloud infrastructure are managed by IT under separate technical standards and the IT Cloud Spend Guidelines.

## 3. Standards

### 3.1 Device categories

IT maintains a hardware catalogue with two main computer categories:

- **Standard laptop:** for most roles in HR, Finance, Legal, Operations, Marketing and non-engineering IT roles.
- **Engineering workstation:** a high-performance laptop or desktop for software engineering, data and design roles, assigned on department manager request.

Each device is issued with a docking station, one external monitor, keyboard, mouse and headset as standard accessories.

### 3.2 Budgets

The standard laptop budget is USD 1,800; engineering workstations USD 3,200. These budgets cover the device and a 3-year warranty but exclude standard accessories. A configuration that exceeds the budget for its category requires department manager approval and a written business justification in the purchase request.

### 3.3 Refresh cycle

Standard laptops are refreshed every 36 months; engineering workstations every 30 months. IT notifies employees about 60 days before their device becomes eligible. Devices are replaced earlier only if they fail and cannot be repaired economically, as assessed by IT. Monitors and accessories are replaced only when faulty.

### 3.4 Purchasing

All hardware must be purchased through IT; personal purchases are not reimbursed. IT raises the purchase request in the procurement system, using approved hardware vendors, and the request is approved according to the Purchase Approval Matrix. Bulk refresh orders follow the quote and tender requirements of the Acme Procurement Policy; they may not be split into smaller orders to stay below a threshold. Purchasing cards may not be used to buy computers or mobile devices.

### 3.5 Configuration and security

Every device is enrolled in Acme's device management system, encrypted and configured with the standard security baseline before it is handed over. Employees may not remove security software or change the baseline configuration.

### 3.6 Returns and disposal

When a device is replaced or an employee leaves Acme, the device must be returned to IT within 5 business days. Retired devices are wiped and recycled under the IT Asset Disposal Procedure. Employees may not keep or sell Acme hardware.

### 3.7 Lost or stolen devices

Lost or stolen devices must be reported to IT within 24 hours so the device can be locked and wiped remotely. A replacement is issued from IT stock where available.

## 4. Responsibilities

- **Employees** use devices with care, keep them secure and return them when required.
- **Department managers** request engineering workstations where justified and approve hardware purchase requests for their teams.
- **IT** maintains the catalogue, raises hardware purchase requests, configures and supports devices, and manages refresh and disposal.
- **Finance** approves hardware requests above the Finance threshold and tracks hardware as fixed assets where applicable.

## 5. Exceptions

Non-standard devices, such as a specific model needed for accessibility reasons or for testing Acme products, may be approved by the head of IT and the department manager on written request. Accessibility-related requests are prioritised and are never refused on cost grounds alone. Exceptions do not change the requirement that all hardware is purchased through IT.

# Acme Procurement Policy

**Effective date:** 2026-01-01
**Owning department:** Company-wide
**Classification:** INTERNAL

## 1. Purpose

This policy sets out how Acme Corp buys goods and services. It exists to make sure that every purchase is necessary, competitively priced, properly approved, paid from the correct budget and made with a vendor that Acme has vetted. It is the parent policy for all other procurement procedures and guidelines.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to all employees, contractors and temporary staff in every department (IT, HR, Finance, Legal, Operations and Marketing) who request, approve, order or receive goods and services on behalf of Acme. It covers one-time purchases, recurring services, software subscriptions, professional services and capital equipment, regardless of how the purchase is ultimately paid.

## 3. Purchasing Rules

### 3.1 Purchase requests

All purchases of goods and services must be raised as a purchase request in the procurement system. A purchase request must include:

- a clear description of the goods or services and the business need;
- the total expected cost in USD, including taxes, delivery, installation and the full term of any recurring charges;
- the selected vendor and the budget or cost centre to be charged;
- supporting quotes or tender documents where required by section 3.2.

No commitment may be made to a vendor, verbally or in writing, until the purchase request has been fully approved and a purchase order has been issued. Invoices received without a purchase order will be returned to the vendor.

### 3.2 Competitive sourcing

The level of competition required depends on the total value of the purchase:

| Total purchase value | Sourcing requirement |
|---|---|
| Up to USD 25,000 | One written quote; requesters are encouraged to compare prices |
| Above USD 25,000 up to USD 250,000 | At least three written quotes |
| Above USD 250,000 | Competitive tender managed by the Procurement Operations team |

At least three written quotes are required for purchases above USD 25,000. A competitive tender is required for purchases above USD 250,000. Quotes must be comparable in scope and no more than 90 days old when the request is submitted. Where fewer than three quotes can be obtained, the requester must document why in the purchase request.

### 3.3 Approved vendors

Purchases must use an approved (active) vendor; suspended vendors may not receive new orders. A vendor that is not yet in the vendor master must complete the Vendor Onboarding Procedure before any order is placed. Requesters may check a vendor's status in the procurement system before raising a request.

### 3.4 Approval

Each purchase request is routed for approval according to the Purchase Approval Matrix. In summary, requests up to and including USD 1,000 are approved automatically, requests above USD 1,000 need department manager approval, requests above USD 10,000 also need Finance approval, and requests above USD 100,000 also need Administrator approval. The Purchase Approval Matrix is the authoritative source for approval routing.

### 3.5 Prohibited practices

- Splitting a purchase into smaller requests to avoid an approval threshold is prohibited. Related items needed for the same project or from the same vendor within a 30-day period must be combined in a single request.
- Raising a purchase request after the goods or services have already been ordered is prohibited, except under the Emergency Purchasing Procedure.
- Accepting personal benefits from a vendor in connection with a purchase is prohibited, as described in the Conflict of Interest Policy.

### 3.6 Payment methods

Low-value purchases may be made with a purchasing card within the limits of the Purchasing Card (P-Card) Policy. All other purchases are paid by Finance against a purchase order after the checks in the Goods Receipt and Three-Way Match procedure.

## 4. Responsibilities

- **Employees (requesters)** identify the need, obtain quotes, raise the purchase request and confirm receipt of goods or services.
- **Department managers** confirm the business need and budget availability and approve requests within their authority.
- **Finance** approves requests above the Finance threshold, maintains the vendor master and processes payments.
- **Administrators** approve the highest-value requests and maintain the configuration of the procurement system.
- **The Procurement Operations team** runs competitive tenders, supports quote comparisons and monitors compliance with this policy.

## 5. Exceptions

Exceptions to the quote or tender requirements (for example, a sole-source supplier or a continuation of existing proprietary systems) must be justified in writing in the purchase request and approved by Finance in addition to the normal approval chain. Exceptions do not remove the need for a purchase request or for approval under the Purchase Approval Matrix. Genuine emergencies are handled under the Emergency Purchasing Procedure.

Breaches of this policy may result in the purchase being cancelled, the cost being charged back to the department, and disciplinary action.

# Recruitment Agency Engagement

**Effective date:** 2025-09-15
**Owning department:** HR
**Classification:** INTERNAL

## 1. Purpose

This policy sets the rules for engaging external recruitment agencies and executive search firms at Acme Corp. It controls recruitment costs, ensures agencies are engaged on consistent commercial terms, and protects candidate personal data.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to all hiring for permanent roles in every department. It covers contingency recruitment, retained executive search and recruitment process outsourcing. Temporary staffing and contractor agencies are covered by the Acme Procurement Policy and are outside the fee rules in section 3.3.

## 3. Rules

### 3.1 Who may engage an agency

- Only HR Talent Acquisition may engage a recruitment agency. Department managers and other employees must not brief, sign terms with or send vacancy details to an agency directly.
- An agency is used only when HR confirms that direct sourcing has been tried for at least 15 business days, or when the role is senior or highly specialised.

### 3.2 Approved agencies

- Agencies must be approved (active) vendors on HR's preferred supplier list. New agencies follow the Vendor Onboarding Procedure.
- Agencies process candidate personal data and must sign the Data Processing Addendum before receiving any vacancy or candidate information.
- No more than 3 agencies may be briefed on the same vacancy at the same time.

### 3.3 Fees

- Agency fees are capped at 18% of first-year base salary.
- The fee is calculated on base salary only. Bonuses, commission, equity, allowances and benefits are excluded.
- Retained executive search fees are also subject to the cap and may be paid in up to three instalments: on engagement, on shortlist and on the candidate's start date.
- Agencies may not charge advertising, administration or expense fees unless agreed in writing by HR before the search begins.

### 3.4 Terms of business

- All agencies must sign Acme's standard recruitment terms, available from Legal. Agency terms of business are not accepted.
- The standard terms include a candidate ownership period of 6 months from first introduction.
- The standard terms include a rebate if the hired candidate leaves within 90 days of starting: a full refund within the first 30 days and a 50% refund between 31 and 90 days, or a free replacement at HR's choice.
- CVs sent without a written brief from HR create no fee obligation.

### 3.5 Purchase orders and invoices

- HR raises a purchase request when the candidate accepts an offer. The value is the calculated fee.
- The request follows the Purchase Approval Matrix: requests above USD 1,000 require department manager approval, and requests above USD 10,000 additionally require Finance approval.
- The candidate's confirmed start date in the HR system serves as the goods receipt. Invoices are paid only after a three-way match of purchase order, goods receipt and invoice.

### 3.6 Candidate experience and conduct

- Agencies must represent Acme accurately and must not share salary ranges or vacancy details beyond the agreed brief.
- Agencies must not approach current Acme employees for roles elsewhere for 12 months after their last placement with Acme.
- Agencies must follow the Supplier Code of Conduct, including fair and non-discriminatory treatment of candidates.

## 4. Performance Review

HR reviews each preferred agency every 6 months on time to shortlist, interview-to-offer ratio, offer acceptance rate and 90-day retention. Agencies that do not place a candidate within 12 months are removed from the preferred supplier list.

## 5. Responsibilities

- **Employees** refer any agency contact about vacancies to HR.
- **Department managers** define the role requirements, interview candidates and approve the fee purchase request.
- **HR** owns this policy, manages the preferred supplier list, briefs agencies, negotiates terms and raises purchase requests.
- **Finance** approves fees above USD 10,000 and processes rebates.
- **Legal** maintains the standard recruitment terms.

## 6. Exceptions

A fee above the 18% cap may be agreed only for exceptional executive or highly specialised roles, with written approval from HR and Finance before the agency is briefed. Exceptions are recorded against the vacancy and reported to HR leadership quarterly.

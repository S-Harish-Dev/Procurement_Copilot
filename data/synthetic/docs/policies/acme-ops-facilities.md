# Facilities Services Procurement

**Effective date:** 2025-12-15
**Owning department:** Operations
**Classification:** INTERNAL

## 1. Purpose

This policy sets out how Acme Corp buys and manages facilities services for its offices, warehouses and other sites. It ensures that facilities contracts are competitively sourced, that on-site contractors work safely, and that Acme retains the flexibility to change suppliers when service or business needs change.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to all facilities services purchased for any Acme site, including:

- cleaning and janitorial services;
- security guarding and access control services;
- building maintenance, HVAC, electrical, plumbing and lift servicing;
- catering, vending and pantry supplies;
- waste management and recycling;
- landscaping, pest control and snow removal;
- minor fit-out works under USD 100,000.

Property leases and major construction projects are managed by Operations with Legal and are outside this policy.

## 3. Rules

### 3.1 Sourcing

- All facilities services must be raised as a purchase request in the procurement system and use an approved (active) vendor.
- At least three written quotes are required for purchases above USD 25,000, and a competitive tender is required for purchases above USD 250,000. Value is calculated over the full contract term, not per month.
- Services delivered across several sites are sourced as a single contract where practical. Splitting a purchase into smaller requests to avoid an approval threshold is prohibited.
- Waste management vendors must hold the required local waste carrier licences. Preference is given to vendors holding a recognised sustainability certification.

### 3.2 Contract terms

- Facilities contracts include a 30-day termination for convenience clause.
- The standard contract term is 1 to 3 years. Extensions beyond 3 years require a new market test.
- Contracts must include service levels, monthly KPI reporting, a price review mechanism no more frequent than annually, and service credits for missed service levels.
- Contracts above USD 100,000 require Legal review before signature.
- Contract owners must review auto-renewing contracts at least 90 days before the renewal date.

### 3.3 Approvals

Facilities purchases follow the Purchase Approval Matrix. Purchase requests up to and including USD 1,000 are approved automatically. Requests above USD 1,000 require approval by the requester's department manager, requests above USD 10,000 additionally require Finance approval, and requests above USD 100,000 additionally require approval by an Administrator.

### 3.4 On-site contractors

- Contractors must provide an insurance certificate with general liability cover of at least USD 1,000,000 per occurrence before starting work.
- All contractor staff complete Acme's site safety induction before their first visit and sign in and out at reception.
- Work involving heights, hot work, electrical isolation or confined spaces requires a permit to work issued by the site facilities lead.
- Security guarding staff must be licensed where local law requires it.

### 3.5 Performance management

- Operations reviews each contract's KPIs monthly and holds a formal service review with major vendors every quarter.
- A vendor that misses the same service level in two consecutive months receives a written corrective action notice with 30 days to remedy.
- If performance is not restored, Operations may exercise the termination for convenience clause after consulting Legal.

### 3.6 Reactive and emergency work

Urgent repairs follow the Emergency Purchasing Procedure. Emergency purchases may be made before approval only to prevent safety risks or major outages, and a retrospective purchase request must be raised within 2 business days of an emergency purchase.

### 3.7 Invoices

Invoices are paid only after a three-way match of purchase order, goods receipt and invoice. For services, the site facilities lead records a monthly service confirmation in the procurement system as the goods receipt.

## 4. Responsibilities

- **Employees** report facilities issues through the facilities helpdesk and do not engage contractors directly.
- **Department managers** in Operations own facilities contracts, approve requests at manager level and run service reviews.
- **Finance** approves requests above USD 10,000 and monitors facilities spend against budget.
- **Legal** reviews contracts above USD 100,000 and advises on termination.
- **Administrators** approve requests above USD 100,000.

## 5. Exceptions

Where a landlord requires the use of a specific contractor under the lease, the competitive quote requirement does not apply. The lease reference is recorded on the purchase request. The termination for convenience clause may be omitted only with written approval from Legal and Finance.

# Payment Run Procedure

**Effective date:** 2026-01-12
**Owning department:** Finance
**Classification:** INTERNAL

## 1. Purpose

This procedure sets out how Acme schedules, prepares, approves and releases payments to vendors. It aims to pay vendors accurately and on time, protect Acme against fraudulent or duplicate payments, and give departments a predictable timetable for when approved invoices will be paid.

## 2. Scope

This procedure applies to all vendor payments made from Acme bank accounts, including payments for goods, services, outside counsel fees and approved refunds to vendors. It does not cover payroll, employee expense reimbursements under the Travel and Expense Policy, or P-Card settlements, which follow their own processes.

## 3. Payment Rules

### 3.1 Payment schedule

- Payment runs occur every Tuesday and Thursday.
- Invoices that are approved and fully matched by 12:00 noon on the business day before a run are included in that run. Invoices approved after the cut-off are paid in the next run.
- When a scheduled run falls on a public holiday, it moves to the next business day.
- Invoices are scheduled for payment on the last run before their due date, so that Acme meets agreed payment terms without paying earlier than required.

### 3.2 Eligibility for payment

An invoice may be included in a payment run only when all of the following conditions are met:

- The invoice relates to a purchase order raised in the procurement system and approved under the Purchase Approval Matrix.
- The three-way match of purchase order, goods receipt and invoice has passed, or any price variance has been approved as described in Goods Receipt and Three-Way Match.
- The vendor is active in the vendor master. Payments to suspended vendors are held until Finance confirms that the obligation is valid.
- The vendor's bank details have been verified. Bank detail changes require written notice and a telephone call-back to a known contact, as set out in the Vendor Onboarding Procedure, and no payment is released to new bank details until the call-back is complete.

### 3.3 Preparing the run

- Finance generates the payment proposal from the procurement system on the morning of each run day.
- The proposal is checked for duplicate invoices (same vendor, invoice number or amount within 30 days), payments to newly changed bank details and payments above USD 100,000.
- Any flagged item is removed from the run until it has been investigated and cleared.

### 3.4 Approval and release

- Every payment run is approved by two Finance team members: one who prepared the proposal and a different person who reviews and releases it. No one may both prepare and release the same run.
- Runs with a total value above USD 500,000 also require release approval by the Finance department manager.
- Payment files are transmitted to the bank by 3:00 pm on the run day. Vendors normally receive funds within 1 to 2 business days.
- Remittance advices are sent automatically to the vendor's registered remittance contact on the day of release.

### 3.5 Urgent and off-cycle payments

- Off-cycle payments are permitted only for emergency purchases under the Emergency Purchasing Procedure, court or regulatory deadlines, or to avoid disruption of a critical service.
- An off-cycle payment request must state the reason and be approved by the Finance department manager. Off-cycle payments follow the same two-person release rule.
- Departments must not promise vendors payment outside the regular schedule without prior agreement from Finance.

### 3.6 Payment holds

- A department manager may ask Finance to place a hold on an invoice where there is a dispute about quality, quantity or price. The reason for the hold must be recorded in the procurement system.
- Holds are reviewed weekly. Holds older than 30 days are escalated to the department manager and, where a contract dispute exists, to Legal.

## 4. Responsibilities

- **Employees** receipt goods and services promptly so invoices can be matched and paid on time.
- **Department managers** approve invoices that need business confirmation and resolve disputes that cause payment holds.
- **Finance** prepares, reviews and releases payment runs, verifies bank details and manages off-cycle payments.
- **Administrators** maintain user access to payment functions so that preparation and release rights are kept separate.

## 5. Exceptions

Payments for which the standard eligibility checks cannot be completed may be released only with the written approval of the Finance department manager, with the reason recorded in the payment file. Exceptions are reviewed monthly by Finance. Suspected fraudulent payment requests must be reported immediately to the Chief Compliance Officer.

# IT Asset Disposal Procedure

**Effective date:** 2025-08-01
**Owning department:** IT
**Classification:** INTERNAL

## 1. Purpose

This procedure describes how Acme Corp retires and disposes of IT equipment securely, legally and sustainably. It prevents data leakage from retired devices, keeps the IT asset register accurate and ensures that electronic waste is handled responsibly.

## 2. Scope

This procedure applies to all IT equipment owned or leased by Acme in every department, including laptops, engineering workstations, desktops, monitors, mobile phones, tablets, servers, network equipment, printers and removable storage media. It applies to all employees, department managers and IT staff who hold, collect or dispose of such equipment.

## 3. Procedure

### 3.1 When a device is retired

A device is retired when:

- it reaches the end of its refresh cycle (standard laptops are refreshed every 36 months; engineering workstations every 30 months);
- it is beyond economical repair, as assessed by IT;
- it is returned by a departing employee and is not suitable for redeployment; or
- a leased device reaches the end of its lease.

### 3.2 Return to IT

- Employees must return retired devices, including chargers and accessories, to IT within 5 business days of receiving a replacement or of their last working day.
- Department managers confirm that all devices assigned to a departing employee have been returned.
- Devices must never be given away, sold or thrown away by the employee or the department.

### 3.3 Asset register

IT updates the asset register when a device is received, recording the asset tag, serial number, condition and intended disposal route. The register status changes from "in use" to "retired" and then to "disposed" when the recycler's certificate is received.

### 3.4 Data sanitisation

- Retired devices must be wiped to NIST 800-88 and disposed of through a certified recycler.
- IT performs or supervises the wipe and records the method and result against the asset tag.
- Storage media that cannot be wiped successfully, or that come from devices which held restricted data, are physically destroyed by shredding.
- Leased devices are wiped before return to the lessor.

### 3.5 Disposal routes

| Route | Conditions |
|---|---|
| Redeployment | Device under 3 years old, in good condition, wiped and reimaged |
| Recycling | All other devices, through a certified recycler |
| Lease return | Leased devices, after wiping |

Donation of devices to charities requires approval from IT and Finance and is limited to devices that have been wiped and are fully functional. Sale of devices to employees is not permitted.

### 3.6 Certified recyclers

- Recyclers must hold a recognised electronics recycling certification (such as R2 or e-Stewards) and must be onboarded under the Vendor Onboarding Procedure.
- Each collection is accompanied by an itemised manifest listing asset tags and serial numbers.
- The recycler must provide a certificate of destruction or recycling within 30 days of collection. IT reconciles the certificate against the manifest.
- Recycling services are purchased through a purchase request and follow the Purchase Approval Matrix.

### 3.7 Lost or stolen devices

Lost or stolen devices must be reported to IT within 24 hours. IT triggers a remote wipe where available and marks the device as lost in the asset register.

## 4. Responsibilities

- **Employees** return devices promptly and report lost or stolen devices.
- **Department managers** ensure their team's devices are returned and confirm returns for departing employees.
- **IT** owns this procedure, maintains the asset register, performs wipes and manages recyclers.
- **Finance** removes disposed assets from the fixed asset register and approves donations.
- **Administrators** remove retired devices from device management and system access lists.

## 5. Records

Wipe records, manifests and recycler certificates are stored in the asset management system and kept in line with Procurement Records Retention.

## 6. Exceptions

Devices subject to a legal hold or an investigation must not be wiped or disposed of until Legal releases them. Any other exception requires written approval from IT and must be recorded in the asset register.

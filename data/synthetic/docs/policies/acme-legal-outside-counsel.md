# Outside Counsel Guidelines

**Effective date:** 2026-01-05
**Owning department:** Legal
**Classification:** INTERNAL

## 1. Purpose

These guidelines set the rules for engaging, instructing and paying external law firms on behalf of Acme. They are intended to keep legal spend predictable, make sure matters are staffed appropriately, and give the Legal department full visibility of all external legal work.

## 2. Scope

These guidelines apply to every engagement of outside counsel by any Acme department, including litigation, regulatory advice, employment matters, intellectual property work, transactional support and local-law advice in other jurisdictions. They also apply to any other professional firm that provides legal services, such as patent agents and specialist regulatory advisers.

## 3. Engagement Rules

### 3.1 Who may engage outside counsel

- Only the Legal department may engage outside counsel. Other departments that need external legal advice must submit a request to Legal describing the issue, the business owner and the expected timeline.
- Outside counsel must be selected from Acme's panel of approved law firms. Using a firm outside the panel requires written approval from the head of Legal and the firm must complete the Vendor Onboarding Procedure before work begins.
- Every engagement must be documented in an engagement letter on Acme's standard terms, as described in the Standard Contract Templates Guide.

### 3.2 Budgets and estimates

- Outside counsel must provide a budget estimate before work begins and bill in 6-minute increments.
- The budget estimate must break the matter into phases (for example, initial assessment, pleadings, discovery, trial or completion) and state the expected fees and disbursements for each phase.
- A purchase request must be raised in the procurement system for the estimated value of the matter and approved under the Purchase Approval Matrix before the engagement letter is signed.
- Counsel must notify the responsible Legal team member in writing as soon as it becomes likely that fees will exceed the estimate by more than 10%. Work beyond the approved estimate is not paid unless a revised estimate has been approved and the purchase request amended.

### 3.3 Staffing

- Matters must be staffed with the minimum number of timekeepers needed. No more than one partner and two associates may bill on a matter without prior approval from Legal.
- Acme does not pay for time spent by trainees or paralegals on administrative tasks, or for time spent getting new team members up to speed when counsel changes staffing.
- Attendance of more than one timekeeper at a meeting, call or hearing must be approved in advance.

### 3.4 Billing requirements

- Invoices are issued monthly and must reference the Acme purchase order number and matter name.
- Each time entry must show the date, timekeeper, task description, time in 6-minute increments and the applicable rate. Block billing (several tasks combined into one entry) is not accepted.
- Hourly rates are fixed for the calendar year agreed in the engagement letter. Rate increases require 60 days' written notice and Legal approval, and take effect only from the following 1 January.
- Acme does not pay for overhead costs such as internal photocopying, word processing, local travel, meals during ordinary working hours or legal research database subscriptions.
- Disbursements above USD 1,000 (such as expert fees, court reporters or translators) require prior written approval from Legal.

### 3.5 Invoice review and payment

- The responsible Legal team member reviews every invoice against the estimate and these guidelines within 10 business days of receipt and may reject or reduce non-compliant entries.
- Approved invoices are paid through the normal payment run in line with the Payment Run Procedure.

### 3.6 Confidentiality and conflicts

- Outside counsel must keep all Acme information confidential and comply with the Vendor Data Protection Requirements when handling personal data.
- Counsel must run a conflict check before accepting a matter and disclose any actual or potential conflict immediately.

## 4. Responsibilities

- **Employees and department managers** route all requests for external legal advice through Legal and do not instruct firms directly.
- **Legal** selects counsel, agrees estimates, manages the matter and reviews invoices.
- **Finance** tracks legal spend against the Legal budget, accrues unbilled work at month end and processes payment.
- **Administrators** maintain panel firm records in the procurement system.

## 5. Exceptions

Where urgent advice is needed to protect Acme's legal position (for example, an injunction or a regulatory deadline within 48 hours), Legal may instruct a panel firm before the purchase request is approved. A retrospective purchase request must then be raised within 2 business days, consistent with the Emergency Purchasing Procedure. All other exceptions require written approval from the head of Legal.

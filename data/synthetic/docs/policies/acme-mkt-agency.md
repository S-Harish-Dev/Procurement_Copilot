# Marketing Agency Engagement Rules

**Effective date:** 2026-01-20
**Owning department:** Marketing
**Classification:** INTERNAL

## 1. Purpose

These rules govern how Acme Corp selects, contracts, briefs and pays marketing agencies. They aim to get good value from agency spend, measure agency performance objectively, and make sure Acme owns the creative work it pays for.

## 2. Scope

These rules apply to all agencies providing marketing services to Acme, including creative, digital, media buying, public relations, content, social media, research and branding agencies. They apply to Marketing and to any other department that commissions marketing work. Event organisers and sponsorships are covered by Events and Sponsorship Purchasing.

## 3. Rules

### 3.1 Selecting an agency

- Agencies must be approved (active) vendors. New agencies follow the Vendor Onboarding Procedure.
- At least three written quotes are required for purchases above USD 25,000. A competitive tender (a formal pitch) is required for purchases above USD 250,000, based on the total value over the contract term.
- Agencies are not paid for pitch work unless a pitch fee is agreed in writing before the pitch. Any pitch fee is capped at USD 5,000 per agency.

### 3.2 Engagement models

- **Retainer:** a fixed monthly fee for an agreed scope of ongoing work. Retainer terms are limited to 12 months, with renewal subject to performance.
- **Project:** a fixed fee for a defined deliverable under a statement of work.
- **Rate card:** time-based work at agreed hourly or daily rates. Rate cards are fixed for the contract term.

Every project and every retainer must have a written statement of work describing deliverables, timelines, fees and KPIs before work begins.

### 3.3 Retainer performance

- Agency retainers are reviewed quarterly against agreed KPIs.
- KPIs are set in the statement of work and may include campaign results, delivery against deadlines, budget accuracy and quality of work.
- An agency that misses its KPIs in two consecutive quarterly reviews is placed on a 60-day improvement plan. If performance does not improve, Marketing may reduce the scope or end the retainer under the contract's termination clause.

### 3.4 Intellectual property

- Creative work is owned by Acme on payment; agencies must assign IP in the contract.
- The assignment covers all deliverables, drafts, source files and campaign concepts created for Acme.
- Agencies must obtain and document licences for any third-party content (such as stock imagery, music, fonts or talent rights). Licence terms must cover Acme's intended use, territory and duration, and copies are provided to Marketing.
- Agencies may show Acme work in their portfolio only with Marketing's written consent.

### 3.5 Third-party costs and media

- Third-party costs such as printing, production and paid media are passed through at cost unless a markup is agreed in the statement of work.
- Agency commission on media buying is capped at 10% of media spend.
- Media plans above USD 50,000 must be approved by the department manager in Marketing before booking, and media invoices must be supported by third-party proof of delivery.

### 3.6 Contracts, approvals and payment

- Agency contracts are raised as a purchase request and follow the Purchase Approval Matrix based on the annual value. Requests above USD 10,000 additionally require Finance approval, and requests above USD 100,000 additionally require approval by an Administrator.
- Contracts above USD 100,000 require Legal review before signature.
- Agencies may not subcontract work without Acme's written consent.
- Invoices are paid only after the deliverable or monthly service is confirmed as received in the procurement system.

### 3.7 Data and brand protection

Agencies that receive customer or prospect personal data must sign the Data Processing Addendum before access is granted. Agencies must follow Acme brand guidelines and must not publish anything on Acme's behalf without Marketing's approval.

## 4. Responsibilities

- **Employees** in Marketing brief agencies, confirm receipt of deliverables and track KPIs.
- **Department managers** in Marketing approve statements of work, run quarterly reviews and approve media plans.
- **Finance** approves higher-value requests and monitors agency spend against budget.
- **Legal** drafts and reviews agency contracts, including IP assignment terms.
- **Administrators** approve requests above USD 100,000.

## 5. Exceptions

Urgent work may start on a signed short-form statement of work limited to USD 10,000 while the full contract is completed. The IP assignment requirement cannot be waived. Any other exception requires written approval from the Marketing department manager and Finance.

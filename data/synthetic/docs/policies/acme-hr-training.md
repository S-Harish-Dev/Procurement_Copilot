# Training and Development Purchasing

**Effective date:** 2026-01-01
**Owning department:** HR
**Classification:** INTERNAL

## 1. Purpose

This policy explains how Acme Corp funds and purchases training, courses, certifications and conferences for employees. It makes development funding fair and predictable, ensures training purchases follow standard procurement controls, and protects Acme's investment in high-cost programmes.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to all employees in every department and covers:

- external courses, workshops and bootcamps;
- professional certifications and exam fees;
- conference registration fees;
- individual online learning purchases.

Company-wide learning platforms and in-house training programmes are purchased centrally by HR and are outside the individual allowance. Travel to training events is covered by the Travel and Expense Policy.

## 3. Rules

### 3.1 Annual allowance

- Each employee has an annual training allowance of USD 1,500.
- The allowance runs on the fiscal year and does not carry over. Unused amounts expire at year end.
- Employees who join part-way through the year receive a pro-rata allowance based on full months remaining.
- Course fees, exam fees and conference registration fees count against the allowance. Travel, hotel and meal costs do not.

### 3.2 Requesting training

- Employees submit a training request to their department manager at least 15 business days before the course start date, describing the course, provider, cost and expected benefit.
- After the department manager agrees the request, the purchase is raised as a purchase request in the procurement system.
- Purchase requests up to and including USD 1,000 are approved automatically; above USD 1,000 they require department manager approval, and above USD 10,000 they additionally require Finance approval, in line with the Purchase Approval Matrix.
- Course fees of up to USD 2,500 may instead be paid with a P-Card where the provider accepts card payment. Travel to the course may not be booked on a P-Card.

### 3.3 Costs above the allowance

Training that costs more than the remaining allowance may be funded from the department's budget if the department manager confirms it is needed for the employee's current or planned role. HR keeps a record of all department-funded training.

### 3.4 Retention agreement

- External training above USD 3,000 requires a 12-month retention agreement.
- The agreement must be signed by the employee before the purchase request is submitted.
- If the employee resigns or is dismissed for cause within 12 months of completing the training, the employee repays the training cost on a pro-rata basis for each full month remaining.
- Repayment does not apply when Acme ends employment for redundancy or when the employee leaves for documented health reasons.

### 3.5 Providers

- Training providers paid by purchase order must be approved (active) vendors in the procurement system. New providers follow the Vendor Onboarding Procedure.
- Online learning subscriptions are recurring SaaS purchases and require an IT security review before purchase. They may not be paid with a P-Card.
- Employees must not accept free places, discounts or gifts from training providers that are bidding for Acme business. Gifts above USD 100 in value must be declared under the Conflict of Interest Policy.

### 3.6 Completion and evidence

Employees upload a certificate of completion or attendance to the HR system within 30 days of finishing the training. If an employee does not attend without good reason, the department manager may charge the cost against the next year's allowance.

## 4. Responsibilities

- **Employees** plan their development, submit requests on time, sign retention agreements where required and provide evidence of completion.
- **Department managers** assess business need, approve requests and decide on department funding above the allowance.
- **HR** owns this policy, tracks allowance balances, manages retention agreements and purchases central learning platforms.
- **Finance** approves training purchase requests above USD 10,000 and processes any repayments.

## 5. Exceptions

Mandatory compliance or safety training required by law or by a customer contract is funded by the department and does not count against the allowance. Any other exception, including carrying over an allowance because of approved leave, requires approval from HR and the department manager.

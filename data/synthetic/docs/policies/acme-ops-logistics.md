# Logistics and Freight Procurement

**Effective date:** 2026-02-20
**Owning department:** Operations
**Classification:** INTERNAL

## 1. Purpose

This policy sets out how Acme Corp buys inbound, outbound and inter-site transport, warehousing and related logistics services. It aims to secure reliable service at competitive rates, make freight costs predictable, and ensure freight invoices are checked against agreed rates before payment.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to all purchases of:

- road, rail, ocean and air freight;
- parcel and courier services;
- third-party warehousing and fulfilment;
- customs brokerage and freight forwarding.

It applies to Operations and to any department that books freight. Employee travel and relocation services are outside this policy.

## 3. Rules

### 3.1 Approved carriers

- Freight may be booked only with approved (active) carriers and logistics providers. New providers follow the Vendor Onboarding Procedure, including the risk review.
- Carriers must hold valid operating licences and cargo liability insurance of at least USD 100,000 per shipment.
- Carriers moving hazardous materials must hold the required dangerous goods certifications, which Operations checks at onboarding and every 12 months.

### 3.2 Contracted rates

- Contracted lanes are tendered at least every 2 years. At least three written quotes are required for purchases above USD 25,000, and a competitive tender is required for purchases above USD 250,000, based on the expected annual spend.
- Rate cards are agreed per lane and service level and fixed for the contract term, except for the fuel surcharge.
- Freight contracts must include fuel surcharge bands tied to a published diesel index. The surcharge is recalculated monthly from the index average of the previous month, using bands of USD 0.10 per gallon. Carriers may not apply any other fuel-related charge.
- Accessorial charges (such as waiting time, liftgate or redelivery) must be listed in the rate card. Unlisted charges are not payable.
- Contracts above USD 100,000 require Legal review before signature.

### 3.3 Spot bookings

- Spot freight (shipments on uncontracted lanes or above contracted capacity) is booked only through Operations.
- Spot shipments above USD 5,000 require at least two quotes from approved carriers, recorded on the booking.
- Spot spend on any lane that exceeds USD 25,000 in a quarter must be reviewed by Operations for inclusion in the next tender.

### 3.4 Approvals

Contracted freight is covered by a blanket purchase order for each carrier, sized to the expected annual spend and approved under the Purchase Approval Matrix. Requests above USD 1,000 require department manager approval, requests above USD 10,000 additionally require Finance approval, and requests above USD 100,000 additionally require approval by an Administrator. Spot bookings outside a blanket purchase order are raised as individual purchase requests.

### 3.5 Freight invoice audit

- Every freight invoice is audited against the rate card, the fuel surcharge band for the shipment month and proof of delivery.
- Price variances above 5% or USD 500 require buyer approval before payment.
- Invoices received more than 90 days after delivery are reviewed by Finance before payment.
- Duplicate invoices and charges for unlisted accessorials are rejected and returned to the carrier.

### 3.6 Carrier performance

Operations measures each contracted carrier monthly on on-time delivery (target at least 95%), damage and loss claims, invoice accuracy and tracking compliance. Carriers below target for two consecutive months receive a corrective action plan; continued underperformance leads to lanes being reallocated at the next tender.

### 3.7 Customs

Customs brokers act under a power of attorney signed by Legal. Brokers must provide entry documents for every import within 5 business days so that duties can be reconciled.

## 4. Responsibilities

- **Employees** book freight only through Operations and confirm delivery in the procurement system.
- **Department managers** in Operations own carrier relationships, approve requests at manager level and run performance reviews.
- **Finance** approves requests above USD 10,000, pays audited invoices and reviews late invoices.
- **Legal** reviews freight contracts and customs powers of attorney.
- **Administrators** approve requests above USD 100,000.

## 5. Exceptions

Where a shipment must move urgently to prevent a production stoppage or safety risk, the Emergency Purchasing Procedure applies, and a retrospective purchase request must be raised within 2 business days. The fuel surcharge requirement cannot be waived for contracted freight.

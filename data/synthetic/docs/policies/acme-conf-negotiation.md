# Negotiation Playbook for Strategic Vendors

**Effective date:** 2026-02-05
**Owning department:** Finance
**Classification:** CONFIDENTIAL

## 1. Purpose

This playbook gives Acme negotiators a common approach and a set of target positions for negotiations with strategic vendors. It is designed to improve commercial outcomes, support the FY2026 procurement savings target, and make sure that concessions are traded deliberately rather than given away.

## 2. Scope

The playbook applies to negotiations of new contracts, renewals and material amendments with strategic vendors. A vendor is classed as strategic when it meets at least one of the following criteria:

- Annual spend with Acme above USD 250,000.
- Sole or single source for a service critical to operations.
- Access to Acme customer data or core systems.

Negotiations with non-strategic vendors may use this playbook as guidance, but its target positions are not mandatory for them.

## 3. Negotiation Rules and Target Positions

### 3.1 Preparation

Before the first negotiation meeting, the lead negotiator must prepare a negotiation plan covering:

- The current spend baseline and the savings target for the category.
- Acme's target position, acceptable fallback and walk-away point for price, payment terms, liability, term length and service levels.
- Market benchmarks and at least two credible alternatives, or a documented explanation if no alternative exists.
- The vendor's current risk tier from the vendor risk register.

The plan is reviewed by Finance and, where non-standard contract terms are expected, by Legal.

### 3.2 Negotiation team

- Every negotiation with a strategic vendor has a named lead negotiator from Finance and a business owner from the requesting department.
- At least two Acme representatives attend every negotiation meeting. No single person may agree commercial terms alone.
- Anyone with a financial interest in the vendor must declare it before participating, in line with the Conflict of Interest Policy.

### 3.3 Target positions

| Term | Target | Fallback | Walk-away |
|---|---|---|---|
| Price | At least 8% below baseline for renewals | 4% below baseline | Any increase above published inflation |
| Payment terms | Net 60 | Net 45 with a price concession of at least 2% | Shorter than Net 45 |
| Contract term | 3 years with fixed pricing | 2 years with a capped annual increase of 3% | Uncapped annual increases |
| Termination | 30 days for convenience | 60 days for convenience | No termination for convenience |
| Liability cap | 2x annual contract value | 1x annual contract value | Below 1x annual contract value |

### 3.4 Payment terms

- Target payment terms are Net 60 for strategic vendors; accept Net 45 only for a price concession of at least 2%.
- Early-payment discounts may be offered to vendors only where Finance confirms that the discount exceeds Acme's cost of funds.
- Payment terms shorter than Net 45 require approval from the Finance department manager.

### 3.5 Trading concessions

- Concessions must always be traded for something of equal or greater value. Volume commitments, longer terms or case-study rights should be offered only in exchange for price, term or service improvements.
- Volume commitments must not exceed 80% of forecast demand.
- Acme's budget figures, savings targets and internal approval limits must never be disclosed to a vendor.

### 3.6 Approval and documentation

- Agreed terms are recorded in a negotiation summary, attached to the purchase request in the procurement system.
- The resulting purchase request is approved under the Purchase Approval Matrix. Contracts above USD 100,000 require Legal review before signature.
- Strategic vendor contracts above USD 250,000 must also show evidence of the competitive tender required by the Acme Procurement Policy, or an approved exception.

## 4. Responsibilities

- **Lead negotiator (Finance)** prepares the plan, leads discussions and records the outcome.
- **Business owner (employee or department manager)** defines requirements and service levels and confirms the deal meets operational needs.
- **Legal** reviews non-standard terms and drafts contract changes.
- **Finance** validates savings and confirms payment-term decisions.
- **Administrators** ensure negotiated prices and terms are loaded into the procurement system.

## 5. Exceptions

Departures from walk-away positions require written approval from Finance and, for legal terms, from Legal, before the deal is agreed. Where a vendor is the only source for a critical service, Finance may approve terms outside the table, and the reason is recorded in the negotiation summary.

## 6. Confidentiality

This playbook, negotiation plans and walk-away positions are confidential and must not be shared with vendors or external advisers without Finance approval.

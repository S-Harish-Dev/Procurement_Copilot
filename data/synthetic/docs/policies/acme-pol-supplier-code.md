# Supplier Code of Conduct

**Effective date:** 2025-01-01
**Owning department:** Company-wide
**Classification:** PUBLIC

## 1. Purpose

Acme Corp is committed to conducting business lawfully, ethically and responsibly, and it expects the same of the companies it works with. This Supplier Code of Conduct sets out the minimum standards that every supplier must meet when providing goods or services to Acme.

## 2. Scope

The Code applies to all suppliers, vendors, contractors, consultants and agencies that do business with Acme, together with their employees, agents and subcontractors involved in supplying Acme. Suppliers are expected to apply equivalent standards to their own supply chains. Acceptance of this Code is a condition of the Vendor Onboarding Procedure, and it forms part of Acme's purchase orders and contracts.

## 3. Standards

### 3.1 Labour and human rights

- Suppliers must prohibit forced and child labour throughout their supply chain. This includes prison labour, bonded labour, human trafficking and the retention of workers' identity documents.
- No worker may be employed below the age of 15, or below the local legal minimum working age if higher. Workers under 18 must not perform hazardous work.
- Working hours must comply with local law and must not exceed 60 hours per week, including overtime, except in genuine emergencies.
- Wages must meet at least the legal minimum and be paid on time.
- Workers must be free to join or not join trade unions and must not face discrimination, harassment or abuse.

### 3.2 Health and safety

Suppliers must provide a safe and healthy workplace, including appropriate protective equipment, training, emergency preparedness and safe accommodation where housing is provided. Suppliers working on Acme premises must follow Acme's site safety rules.

### 3.3 Business integrity

- Suppliers must not offer or pay bribes, kickbacks or facilitation payments to anyone, including Acme employees and public officials.
- Suppliers must not offer Acme employees gifts or hospitality intended to influence a decision. Acme employees are bound by the Conflict of Interest Policy, which limits the gifts they may accept, and they may not accept gifts during an active tender.
- Suppliers must disclose any family or financial relationship with an Acme employee involved in their selection.
- Suppliers must comply with competition law and must not collude with other bidders.

### 3.4 Reporting concerns

Suppliers must report suspected bribery to Acme's ethics hotline within 5 business days. The ethics hotline also accepts reports of any other suspected breach of this Code, and reports may be made anonymously where local law permits. Acme does not tolerate retaliation against anyone who raises a concern in good faith, and suppliers must offer the same protection to their own workers.

### 3.5 Environment

Suppliers must comply with applicable environmental laws, hold required permits, and work to reduce energy use, emissions, water use and waste. Acme encourages suppliers to obtain a recognised sustainability certification, as described in the Sustainable Procurement Guidelines.

### 3.6 Data protection and confidentiality

Suppliers must protect Acme's confidential information and any personal data they handle. Suppliers that process personal data on Acme's behalf must also meet the Vendor Data Protection Requirements.

### 3.7 Trade compliance

Suppliers must comply with applicable export control, sanctions and customs laws and must provide accurate country-of-origin information.

## 4. Responsibilities

- **Suppliers** comply with this Code, communicate it to relevant staff and subcontractors, and keep records that demonstrate compliance.
- **Acme employees** who manage supplier relationships raise concerns about supplier conduct promptly.
- **Finance** confirms acceptance of the Code during vendor onboarding.
- **Legal** investigates reported breaches and decides on remedial action.

## 5. Monitoring and Non-compliance

Acme may request information, questionnaires or on-site audits to verify compliance, with reasonable notice of at least 10 business days for planned audits. Where a breach is found, Acme will normally agree a corrective action plan with the supplier. Serious breaches, such as forced labour, child labour or bribery, or a failure to complete corrective actions, may lead to suspension of the vendor, after which it may not receive new orders, and to termination of contracts.

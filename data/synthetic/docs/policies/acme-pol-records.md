# Procurement Records Retention

**Effective date:** 2025-06-01
**Owning department:** Company-wide
**Classification:** INTERNAL

## 1. Purpose

This policy sets out how long Acme Corp keeps procurement records, where they are stored, and how they are destroyed at the end of their retention period. Consistent retention supports financial audits, tax reviews, contract disputes and investigations, and ensures that Acme does not keep records, including vendor personal data, for longer than necessary.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to all employees, department managers, Finance, Legal and Administrators who create, approve or store procurement records in any department (IT, HR, Finance, Legal, Operations and Marketing). A procurement record is any document or system entry that evidences how a purchase was requested, approved, ordered, received or paid, including:

- purchase requests and their approval history;
- quotes, tender submissions and evaluation scoresheets;
- purchase orders, goods receipts and invoices;
- contracts, amendments, statements of work and renewal notices;
- vendor onboarding documents, risk reviews and security reviews;
- conflict of interest and gift declarations linked to a purchase.

## 3. Retention Rules

### 3.1 Standard retention period

Procurement records are retained for 7 years after contract end. Where a contract is extended or renewed, the retention period starts from the end of the final term, not the original term.

### 3.2 Records without a contract

For purchases made without a formal contract (for example, a one-off purchase order or a P-Card transaction), the retention period of 7 years starts from the date of final payment.

### 3.3 Specific record types

| Record type | Retention period |
|---|---|
| Contracts, POs, receipts, invoices | 7 years after contract end |
| Winning tender submission | 7 years after contract end |
| Unsuccessful tender submissions | 3 years after contract award |
| Rejected or withdrawn purchase requests | 2 years after rejection or withdrawal |
| Vendor onboarding documents (tax form, bank confirmation, insurance) | 7 years after the vendor is deactivated |
| Approval history in the procurement system | 7 years after contract end |

### 3.4 Storage

- Records must be stored in the procurement system or the contract repository managed by Legal. Personal drives, local laptop folders and chat threads are not acceptable record stores.
- Paper originals must be scanned within 10 business days of receipt. After a quality check, the paper copy may be destroyed unless Legal requires the original (for example, a signed deed).
- Records must not be edited after approval. Corrections are added as a new version with a reason.

### 3.5 Legal holds

Legal may place a hold on any record that is relevant to actual or anticipated litigation, an audit or an investigation. A legal hold suspends destruction regardless of the retention period. Holds stay in force until Legal releases them in writing.

### 3.6 Destruction

- Finance runs a destruction review each January and produces a list of records that have passed their retention period.
- Record owners have 30 days to confirm or object to destruction. Records not under a legal hold are then destroyed.
- Electronic records are permanently deleted; paper records are cross-cut shredded through the approved secure shredding vendor.
- A destruction log (record type, date range and confirmation of destruction) is kept permanently.

## 4. Access to Records

Access follows the classification of the underlying document. Employees can view records for their own requests; department managers can view records for their department; Finance and Administrators can view all procurement records. Records containing bank details or vendor personal data are visible only to Finance and Administrators.

## 5. Responsibilities

- **Employees** ensure that quotes, receipts and supporting documents are attached to the purchase request in the procurement system.
- **Department managers** confirm that their department's records are complete before approving a request and respond to the annual destruction review.
- **Finance** owns this policy, runs the annual destruction review and keeps the destruction log.
- **Legal** manages the contract repository and issues and releases legal holds.
- **Administrators** configure retention settings in the procurement system and review system-level deletion logs.

## 6. Exceptions

Retaining a record beyond its retention period, or destroying it earlier, requires written approval from Finance and Legal. Early destruction is never permitted while a legal hold, audit or investigation is open. Exceptions are recorded in the destruction log.

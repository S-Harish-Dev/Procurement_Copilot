# Events and Sponsorship Purchasing

**Effective date:** 2025-10-15
**Owning department:** Marketing
**Classification:** INTERNAL

## 1. Purpose

This policy sets out how Acme Corp plans, approves and purchases events, trade shows and sponsorships. It ensures that event spend is justified by clear business outcomes, that venue and supplier contracts protect Acme from avoidable costs, and that hospitality and promotional activity stays within Acme's ethical standards.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to:

- trade show and conference exhibition fees;
- event sponsorships, including industry associations and community events;
- customer and partner events hosted by Acme;
- venues, catering, audiovisual services, stand design and event agencies;
- promotional merchandise and giveaways.

It applies to Marketing and to any other department that organises or sponsors an event. Employee travel to events is covered by the Travel and Expense Policy, and internal team events are covered by the Acme Procurement Policy.

## 3. Rules

### 3.1 Planning and business case

- Every event or sponsorship must be included in Marketing's annual events plan or approved as an addition by the Marketing department manager.
- Each event has a budget owner and a brief stating objectives, target audience and expected outcomes, such as qualified leads or pipeline value.
- Event sponsorships above USD 50,000 require a written ROI plan. The ROI plan sets out the total cost (including stand, travel and staff time), measurable targets and how results will be tracked, and is attached to the purchase request.

### 3.2 Purchasing

- All event costs must be raised as a purchase request in the procurement system and follow the Purchase Approval Matrix based on the total event cost. Requests above USD 1,000 require department manager approval, requests above USD 10,000 additionally require Finance approval, and requests above USD 100,000 additionally require approval by an Administrator.
- At least three written quotes are required for purchases above USD 25,000, such as venues, event agencies and stand builders.
- Splitting an event's costs across several requests to avoid an approval threshold is prohibited.
- P-Cards may be used for small on-site costs within the P-Card limits but may not be used for travel or gifts.

### 3.3 Contracts and deposits

- Venue and supplier contracts must state cancellation terms. Cancellation charges must not exceed 50% of the contract value if Acme cancels more than 60 days before the event.
- Deposits paid before the event may not exceed 50% of the contract value.
- Contracts above USD 100,000 require Legal review before signature.
- Contracts must include a force majeure clause that allows cancellation or postponement without penalty.
- Suppliers working on site must provide an insurance certificate before the event.

### 3.4 Hospitality, giveaways and gifts

- Hospitality offered to customers or partners must be modest, directly linked to the event's business purpose and recorded on the event budget.
- Promotional giveaways must not exceed USD 100 in value per recipient.
- Hospitality or gifts must never be offered to public officials, or to employees of a company that is in an active tender with Acme.
- Gifts from vendors above USD 100 in value must be declared and may not be accepted during an active tender.

### 3.5 Prohibited sponsorships

Acme does not sponsor political parties, candidates or campaigns, or organisations whose activities conflict with the Supplier Code of Conduct or Acme's values.

### 3.6 Post-event review

Within 30 days after each event, the budget owner submits a post-event report comparing actual costs and results with the brief. For sponsorships above USD 50,000, the report measures results against the written ROI plan and recommends whether to renew.

## 4. Responsibilities

- **Employees** (budget owners) prepare briefs and ROI plans, raise purchase requests and submit post-event reports.
- **Department managers** in Marketing approve the events plan, approve requests at manager level and review ROI results before renewal.
- **Finance** approves requests above USD 10,000, manages deposits and checks event spend against budget.
- **Legal** reviews contracts above USD 100,000 and sponsorship agreements with unusual terms.
- **Administrators** approve requests above USD 100,000.

## 5. Exceptions

A sponsorship opportunity with a deadline shorter than the normal approval cycle may proceed with a short-form ROI plan, approved by the Marketing department manager and Finance, provided the full ROI plan is completed within 10 business days. No exception is permitted for prohibited sponsorships.
